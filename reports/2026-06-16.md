**🌟 [Illuminating AGN Disks with LISA EMRIs](2026-06-16/01_AGN_Disks_LISA_EMRIs_2606.12531)**
arXiv:[2606.12531](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12531) — Fantocolli, Duque, **Gair** (T3)
*One-line:* First Bayesian LISA forecast with relativistic Teukolsky-based gas torques on EMRIs in Novikov–Thorne disks; separately measures Σ₀ and h₀ without an EM counterpart by breaking the Newtonian Σ/h² degeneracy.

Caveats: only circular equatorial prograde near-plunge orbits; EM "illumination" is asserted rather than computed.

### Should-Read (5)

**🌀 [Universality in the Transition from Inspiral to Plunge](2026-06-16/02_Universality_Inspiral_Plunge_2606.13786)**
arXiv:[2606.13786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13786) — Ribes Metidieri, **Bonga (T2)**, Krishnan, Jaramillo
*One-line:* The Apte–Hughes inclined-Kerr transition equation's physical Painlevé-I solution is identified as the *tritronquée*; the Adali–Tanveer closed-form approximation is plugged in (uniformly bounded, |E| ≤ 6.89×10⁻⁵); the universal appearance of Painlevé I is reframed as crossing a fold branch of a cusp catastrophe; extremal Kerr (χ=1, Q/M²=1/2) is flagged as a possible Painlevé-II regime.
**Quality 7/10 · Relevance 8/10.** *T2-collaborator paper.* Concrete fold/cusp Möbius reductions and Painlevé test are real, not relabelling alone. Limited novelty beyond Apte–Hughes / Compère–Küchler; Stein–Warburton catastrophe work cited but barely engaged.

**🪛 [Modified Teukolsky for EMRIs in Higher-Derivative Gravity](2026-06-16/03_Modified_Teukolsky_HD_Gravity_2606.09766)**
arXiv:[2606.09766](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09766) — C. Yang, Khera, D. Li, **H. Yang (T3)**
*One-line:* First application of the modified Teukolsky formalism (Li–Yagi–Yunes / Hussain–Zimmerman) to a point-particle EMRI source; horizon and ∞ fluxes computed for circular equatorial orbits around a non-rotating BH in parity-preserving cubic gravity, finding a striking 1–2 orders-of-magnitude enhancement in the horizon flux.
**Quality 7/10 · Relevance 8/10.** Schwarzschild-only and no observable phase, but a concrete first MTF-EMRI flux calculation with a clear roadmap to Kerr. The EF/Hawking–Hartle regularization is useful know-how.

**🛡 [Survival of Dark Matter Spikes around Sgr A*](2026-06-16/04_DM_Spike_Survival_2606.13761)**
arXiv:[2606.13761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13761) — Karydas, Scarcella, **B. J. Kavanagh** (≠ Chris Kavanagh), Bertone
*One-line:* 2D anisotropic Fokker–Planck + HaloFeedback master equation + NbodyIMRI: ~82% of DM spike density retained at r ~ 10⁻⁵ pc after NSC + S-stars + ~270 past EMRIs over 10 Gyr; explicitly refutes Sharpe et al. 2026.
**Quality 8/10 · Relevance 8/10.** Sets the prior for DM-dephasing EMRI forecasts — survival means LISA constraints are not nullified. Caveats: GC-specific, no major mergers, IMF-sensitive depletion. The B. J. Kavanagh in author list is the DM phenomenologist (Cantabria/IFCA), not the GR self-force Chris Kavanagh.

**🫧 [Tidal Stripping of Matter Bound to the Secondary](2026-06-16/05_Tidal_Stripping_Secondary_2606.14894)**
arXiv:[2606.14894](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14894) — Nair, Datta
*One-line:* Bound matter on the secondary (gas envelope / DM mini-spike / fallback shell) is tidally stripped during inspiral; even planetary-mass dressings can imprint O(1)–O(10³) rad of dephasing in LISA, via a falsifiable inflection in δϕ̈(t).
**Quality 5/10 · Relevance 8/10.** Crudest possible model (static Hill radius + Hernquist + Newtonian flux); read for the *conceptual framing* (almost all prior work dresses the *primary*, this dresses the secondary), not the quantitative numbers. Natural follow-up: open-subsystem self-force treatment of μ̇.

**〰️ [Dynamical Tidal Response of NS via Scattering Amplitudes](2026-06-16/06_NS_Tidal_Response_Scattering_2606.14405)**

## Papers That Failed Critical Review

**❌ arXiv:[2606.09445](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09445) — Zhong, W.-B. Han et al., *"A self-consistent EOB–Teukolsky framework for generic EMRIs"*.**
The framework is not actually self-consistent: a particle on a *deformed-Kerr* geodesic is fed into the *exact-Kerr* Teukolsky operator, with no gauge/source-matching à la Yunes–Buonanno or Albertini–Buonanno–Khalil–van de Meent. Uncalibrated 3PN A(u), D⁻¹(u) potentials inject conservative O(ν) shifts into Ω_{r,θ,φ} on top of adiabatic Teukolsky fluxes that are already O(ν⁰)-correct — almost certain double-counting, never compared to van de Meent self-force frequency shifts or Pound–Wardell–Warburton–Miller 2nd-order fluxes (Albertini–Buonanno–Khalil–van de Meent not cited at all). "Tens of thousands of modes" is rhetorical (only single-snapshot fluxes shown). Manuscript is typo-heavy ("Furthurmore", "nessasary", "geodeisc", "in principe", "callibrated"). **Quality 4/10.** Worth a skim only to track what the Han group is putting out and the new JH homogeneous solver references.

**❌ arXiv:[2606.15381](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15381) — Wu, Luo, Stepanenko, Shi, *"EMRI Dephasing from a Torsion-Inspired Near-Zone Kerr Deformation"*.**
Authors themselves admit: the linearized Einstein–Cartan + Weyssenhoff field equations they derive do *not* produce the 1/r³ metric ansatz they then use for the waveform analysis — instead the linearized response gives a mass renormalization + log r + 1/r² tail. The h_{tφ} = −aα/r⁴ rotating completion is asserted by hand. Their Table 1 honestly shows the fiducial α/M³ = 10⁻³ is 50–100 orders of magnitude above any realistic minimally-coupled EC dark matter. Standard "deformed Kerr + analytic kludge + leading-order quadrupole flux" recipe applied many times before. Raw CAS dumps (e.g. √(Δ²/r⁵) instead of |Δ|/r^{5/2}) suggest minimal review. **Quality 3/10 · Skip.**

## Thematic Trends This Week

**Environmental EMRI physics is heating up.** Five of the six survivors touch on it from different angles: AGN-disk torques (2606.12531), DM-spike survival under stellar perturbations (2606.13761), bound matter on the *secondary* (2606.14894), the modified-Teukolsky machinery that any beyond-vacuum primary will eventually need (2606.09766), and the tidal response of matter objects (2606.14405). The community has clearly moved past "do environments matter?" to "which environments matter most and how do we discriminate them with LISA waveform data alone?" Multiple papers now make degeneracy-breaking arguments (Σ/h² for AGN disks; inflection in δϕ̈ for secondary dressing). The Sharpe-vs-Karydas debate over EMRI-induced DM-spike depletion is the kind of empirical disagreement that needs to be resolved before LISA launches.

**Catastrophe theory and integrable-systems language are reaching the EMRI transition-to-plunge problem.** The Bonga–Krishnan–Jaramillo paper joins the Apte–Hughes / Compère–Küchler / Stein–Warburton line in framing the transition as a singularity in an extended parameter space. The tritronquée identification and the Adali–Tanveer closed form are concrete deliverables that ought to make their way into FEW pipelines. The extremal-Kerr Painlevé-II conjecture is a clean open problem.

**The "deformed-Kerr + analytic-kludge" template continues to flood gr-qc.** Two such papers (2606.09445 and 2606.15381) appeared this week — both with severe internal-consistency issues (uncalibrated EOB ν-corrections layered on Teukolsky; 1/r³ ansatz inconsistent with the field equations claimed to motivate it). The contrast with the careful gauge-invariant amplitude-matching of Saketh–Ghosh–Andersson is instructive: there is a real distinction between "we picked a metric deformation, computed a kludge waveform, claimed a dephasing" and "we derived an observable response function from a controlled EFT matching." The former is becoming saturated noise; the latter still has structural payoff.
