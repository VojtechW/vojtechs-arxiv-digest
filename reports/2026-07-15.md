## 🎯 Reading Priority

### 🌟 Must-Read

**📜 First 1PA scalar-charge EMRI/IMRI systematics** — Barsanti, Burke, Maselli, Sotiriou, Spiers, Warburton, [2607.09310](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09310) (Q 8/10, R 9/10)
First beyond-GR EMRI/IMRI pipeline combining 1PA gravitational self-force with 0PA scalar emission. Bayesian injection–recovery shows scalar charge d is robust to missing 1PA GR corrections but pure-GR templates on scalar-charged signals produce biased posteriors with underestimated errors. Revises down secondary-spin measurability at q ≤ 10⁻⁴, correcting Fisher-based optimism. Restricted to quasi-circular Schwarzschild.
*Tier-2 co-authors: Barsanti, Burke, Spiers, Warburton; Tier-3: Sotiriou.*
[Full report](2026-07-15/01_Barsanti_et_al_1PA_scalar_charge_IMRI_EMRI_2607.09310)

**💥 Star–Disk Collisions II: 3D tidal-domain simulations of QPE debris streams** — Yao, Quataert, Jiang, Linial, [2607.08823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08823) (Q 8/10, R 9/10)
First 3D `Athena++` runs of star–disk collisions around a 10⁶ M☉ SMBH self-consistently including tides and disk rotation. Confirms Linial+2025's triaxial-stream self-similarity, reproduces observed ~10–20% QPE duty cycle from t_dur ~ 2R₁/v_★, and predicts ~10⁻⁴ M☉/collision unbound outflows relevant to slow TDE-like winds. Circular orbits only; no radiative transfer.
*Suková–Zajaček–**Witzany**–Karas 2021 cited as foundational; QPE community reference for the next year.*
[Full report](2026-07-15/02_Yao_Quataert_Jiang_Linial_StarDisk_QPE_II_2607.08823)

### 🌠 Should-Read

**〰️ High-order gravitational late-time tails in Kerr** — Casals, Kavanagh, Neef, Ottewill, [2607.12151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12151) (Q 8/10, R 8/10)
Analytic extension of the Casals–Ottewill MST small-frequency programme to the s=−2 Teukolsky Green function in subextremal Kerr — three orders, finite radius plus H⁺ and ℐ⁺, explicit log corrections and m-dependent horizon anomaly. Coefficients are new for gravitational perturbations; small-ω scattering-data expansions and attached Mathematica notebooks are the practically reusable pieces. Only three orders vs. five for the 2016 scalar case; no engagement with nonlinear-tail literature.
*Tier-2: Kavanagh.*
[Full report](2026-07-15/03_Casals_Kavanagh_Neef_Ottewill_late_time_tails_Kerr_2607.12151)

**🌀 Radiation-hydrodynamics of star–disc collisions** — Janković, Karpov, Zajaček, Karas, Śniegowska, [2607.05508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05508) (Q 7/10, R 9/10)
Prague/Warsaw follow-up to the Suková–Zajaček–**Witzany**–Karas 2021 transit programme: 25+ Phantom SPH runs with grey FLD radiation transport in a local Cartesian box, giving empirical scaling laws for outflow momentum, peak L_bol and flare duration. GSN 069 Monte-Carlo fit favours a solar-mass star on a **retrograde** orbit through a post-TDE disc. Grey LTE cannot reproduce observed spectral temperatures; rigid-sphere elastic-reflection star is not physical; opposite-sign L_peak(Σ) trend to Huang, Linial & Jiang (2025) is unresolved.
*Tier-2 authors: Karas, Zajaček. Prague network.*
[Full report](2026-07-15/04_Jankovic_Karas_Zajacek_star_disc_RHD_QPE_2607.05508)

**🚀 Off-axis relativistic jet from an IMBH — AT 2019ijn / VT J131240+211339** — Levine et al., [2607.10047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10047) (Q 8/10, R 8/10)
Radio + optical + X-ray characterisation of a decade-long transient in a dwarf star-forming galaxy at z=0.273. Two-epoch radio SED modelling favours a narrow (θ_j ≈ 0.1) off-axis jet with E ≈ 2×10⁵² erg — an off-axis AT 2022cmc analogue at the IMBH/dwarf mass scale, with super-Eddington accretion Ṁ/Ṁ_Edd ~ 10³. Single-event classification; BH-stellar-merger origin only disfavoured, not excluded.
[Full report](2026-07-15/05_Levine_offaxis_jet_TDE_IMBH_2607.10047)

### 🔦 Worth-Skimming

**🎇 Twist-free axisymmetric GW near BH threshold** — Adhikari, **Ledvinka**, Cors, Baumgarte, Khirnov, Brügmann, Hilditch, [2607.10843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10843) (Q 8/10, R 4/10)
Three-code (bamps / prague / sphGR) cross-comparison in critical vacuum-GW collapse — off-center Brill waves and Ledvinka–Khirnov time-asymmetric data. Strengthens (does not overturn) the 2023 PRL result that different families give different critical exponents γ and echoing periods Δ. Consensus-building at ~7-digit tuning. Worth 15 minutes for the Prague-network context (Ledvinka, Khirnov both at Charles University).
[Full report](2026-07-15/06_Adhikari_Ledvinka_axisymmetric_GW_critical_2607.10843)

**🎵 QNM contamination by massless scalars (Horndeski EFT)** — Yulo Asuncion, D'Addario, Sotiriou, [2607.08619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08619) (Q 6/10, R 6/10)
Perturbative bookkeeping of shift-symmetric Horndeski to O(εq²) around Kerr: only φG Gauss-Bonnet (α) and G̃₄ (τ₄) survive; QNM "contamination" and "shift" are same-order effects governed by α alone under natural assumptions. LISA SMBH ringdowns are argued to be a poor probe (q ∝ M⁻²); ET/CE stellar-mass BHs better. Ansatz-level only — no numerical δω.
*Tier-3: Sotiriou.*
[Full report](2026-07-15/07_YuloAsuncion_DAddario_Sotiriou_QNM_scalar_contamination_2607.08619)

## 🧩 Papers That Failed Critical Review

**❌ Probing near-zone magnetic fields with EMRIs** — Hu, Du, Li, Zi, [2607.05909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05909). Ernst-background EMRI with neutral secondary and a hybrid RWZ scheme that keeps Schwarzschild wave-propagation potentials while placing the source on Ernst orbits — not a controlled expansion. Headline "B ≈ 10⁹ G required" is a null result in disguise (5–6 orders above realistic values). Cites Polcar–Witzany 2025 but is otherwise light on the established magnetized-orbit dynamics literature (Kološ, Tursunov, Karas, Zelenka, Lukes-Gerakopoulos). Quality 4/10.

**❌ Spinning particle epicyclic frequencies + QPOs in Schwarzschild** — Uktamov, Övgün, Pantig, Ahmedov, [2607.11993](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11993). Linear-in-spin MPD in Schwarzschild is a >30-year-old topic (Suzuki–Maeda 1997; Jefremov et al. 2015). Recompiles r_ISCO expansion, epicyclic frequencies, Lyapunov exponent, and zoom-whirl orbits with no new physics; QPO framing is dimensional-analysis rebranding of the Lyapunov = QNM connection. Cites Witzany–Piovano 2024 as core input but misses the modern spinning-particle self-force literature (Steinhoff, Vines, Ramond, Compère, Skoupý). Övgün-pipeline fingerprint. Quality 3/10.

**❌ GRACE: GPU-accelerated numerical relativity framework** — Musolino, Ecker, Topolski, Cassing, Miler, Ng, Pierre, Most, Rezzolla, [2607.09854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09854). Solid engineering — Z4c + GRMHD-CT + Kokkos + p4est AMR, validated up to magnetized BNS inspiral against FIL — but incremental relative to AthenaK, GRaM-X, and Dendro-GR. No new physics; the differentiator is the p4est AMR backend. Quality 7/10 but outside Vojtěch's critical path.

## 🌐 Thematic Trends This Week

The week's clearest thread is **the maturing star–disk collision framework for QPEs**. Two heavyweight papers from the leading two groups landed nearly simultaneously — Yao, Quataert, Jiang & Linial's 3D `Athena++` Paper II ([2607.08823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08823)) and the Prague/Warsaw radiation-hydrodynamics parameter survey by Janković, Karpov, Zajaček, Karas & Śniegowska ([2607.05508](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05508)). Both build on the Suková–Zajaček–Witzany–Karas 2021 line. Yao et al. resolve stream self-similarity and the duty-cycle universality, while Janković et al. focus on retrograde-orbit inference from GSN 069 lightcurves. The two disagree on the L_peak(Σ_disc) trend — this is the sharpest testable observational signature to watch. Retrograde EMRI capture rates are worth checking against.

**Beyond-GR waveform systematics for LISA** got its first serious 1PA+scalar treatment in Barsanti et al. ([2607.09310](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09310)), a Warburton–Spiers–Sotiriou joint output. The take-home — that scalar charge is robust to 1PA neglect but the reverse test biases everything — is important for LISA data-challenge design. Separately, Yulo Asuncion et al. ([2607.08619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08619)) formalise the Horndeski EFT bookkeeping that produces ringdown "contamination" modes at the same order as frequency shifts — narrowing the parameter freedom in theory-agnostic ringdown tests.

**Analytic Kerr perturbation theory** returned with Casals–Kavanagh–Neef–Ottewill ([2607.12151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12151)) delivering the first three orders of gravitational late-time tails via MST — technically demanding but incremental over the group's 2016 scalar result, and increasingly niche as nonlinear/sourced tails dominate ringdown discussions.

Observationally, the **IMBH-jet population** grew by one: Levine et al. ([2607.10047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10047)) present a nearby off-axis AT 2022cmc analogue in a dwarf galaxy at z=0.273, pushing jet-launching down into the IMBH regime.

Adjacent Prague-network activity: Adhikari, Ledvinka et al. ([2607.10843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10843)) continue building consensus that vacuum critical collapse in axisymmetry has family-dependent γ and Δ — no unique DSS solution — and identify a bamps gauge failure that is a useful numerical-relativity lesson.
