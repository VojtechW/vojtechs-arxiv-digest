### Must-Read

#### 🎵 Resonances as signatures of scalar clouds in eccentric EMRIs

[**arXiv:2605.03756**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03756) — *Xu, Brito, Della Monica, Vicente, Yuan*
One-line: First fully relativistic treatment of *eccentric* EMRIs into superradiantly grown scalar clouds, identifying a dense sequence of strong-field resonances driven by the Ω_r ≠ Ω_φ split that produce detectable GW dephasing.
**Quality 7.5/10 · Relevance 8/10.** Genuinely new strong-field structure with no Newtonian analog; right tool (Schwarzschild–Klein-Gordon perturbations) for the job; adiabatic evolution closes the dephasing loop. Caveats: Schwarzschild only (Kerr is the natural next paper); cloud back-reaction not addressed; scalar→GW flux conversion is implicit.
[Full report](2026-05-12/01_Xu_et_al_resonances_scalar_clouds_eccentric_EMRI_2605.03756)

#### 🌊 HeunC framework for generic Kerr Teukolsky fluxes — benchmarks against pybhpt

[**arXiv:2605.09250**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09250) — *Chen, Cao, Jing*
One-line: Reformulate the radial+angular Teukolsky equations as confluent Heun equations and use connection-coefficient algorithms to eliminate the MST ν-search, yielding 10⁻¹¹ relative-error fluxes 3–10× faster than pybhpt and 2–3× faster than GeneralizedSasakiNakamura.jl over 168-mode sums.
**Quality 7.5/10 · Relevance 9/10.** Direct competitor and potential successor methodology to pybhpt for Teukolsky flux computation — Vojtěch's toolkit. Caveats: code-release status not committed in abstract; "generic orbits" benchmark scope unclear; the marketing flavor of the abstract is unusually strong for a methods paper.
[Full report](2026-05-12/02_Chen_Cao_Jing_HeunC_Kerr_fluxes_2605.09250)

#### 🌀 Spinning charged particles in Schwarzschild + B field

[**arXiv:2605.02084**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02084) — *Shahzadi, Kološ, Zelenka, Lukes-Gerakopoulos*
One-line: First synthesis of the Kološ-Stuchlík-Tursunov magnetised-charged-particle line with the Lukes-Gerakopoulos spinning-particle line — analytical equatorial frequencies and effective potential, plus 4D Poincaré + recurrence-quantification analysis of the non-integrable off-equatorial 3-DOF regime.
**Quality 6.5/10 · Relevance 8/10.** Tier 1 (Lukes-Gerakopoulos) + Tier 2 (Zelenka) collaborators; methodology is the right one for 3-DOF chaos. Caveats: Schwarzschild + Wald rather than Kerr + realistic disc field; MPD SSC choice not flagged in abstract; no observable connection (no GW phasing, no QPO mapping); the conceptual novelty over a decade of similar work is the *combination* rather than any structurally new ingredient.
[Full report](2026-05-12/03_Shahzadi_Kolos_Zelenka_Lukes-Gerakopoulos_spinning_charged_2605.02084)

### Should-Read

#### 〰️ Unifying the RWZ and BPT formalisms on spherical backgrounds

[**arXiv:2605.04147**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04147) — *Pereñíguez*
One-line: Derives the Regge-Wheeler-Zerilli and Bardeen-Press-Teukolsky equations as different projections of a single self-dual curvature equation, making axial/polar isospectrality manifest and enabling algebraic frequency-domain metric reconstruction; ships an xAct notebook.
**Quality 8/10 · Relevance 7/10.** Structurally clean — exactly the kind of formalism cleanup that pays off downstream. Caveats: spherical only (Kerr is the prize); no new physical computation, just framework; time-domain reconstruction obstructions noted but unresolved; immediate astrophysical relevance is indirect.
[Full report](2026-05-12/04_Pereniguez_RWZ_BPT_unification_2605.04147)

#### 🌌 LISA SGWB from eccentric stellar-mass BBHs in vacuum

[**arXiv:2605.05537**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05537) — *Chen, Chandramouli, Pozzoli, Buscicchio, Barausse*
One-line: Bayesian population-level SGWB model for eccentric sBBHs; finds monochromatic e₀ ≳ 0.9 is robustly distinguishable from circular at LISA, thermal distributions inject systematic biases at f_orb = 10⁻⁴ Hz but vanish at 10⁻⁵ Hz; dynamical friction separable only at ρ ≳ 10⁻⁷ g/cm³.
**Quality 7/10 · Relevance 6/10.** Solid technical work; LISA-to-ground-detector eccentricity bound is a genuine new deliverable. Caveats: vacuum baseline ignores channel-dependent eccentricity-density correlations; conclusions are highly sensitive to the f_orb of injection (a channel-dependent quantity); "thermal eccentricity is indistinguishable from circular at 10⁻⁵ Hz" is a non-result wearing a positive-spin abstract.
[Full report](2026-05-12/05_Chen_et_al_LISA_SGWB_eccentric_sBBH_2605.05537)

#### 🛞 High-spin BBH subpopulation from AGN accretion

[**arXiv:2605.09351**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09351) — *Bartos, Haiman*
One-line: Three-component mixture model with theoretically pinned shapes fitted to 166 LVK BBHs finds ln B = 5.7 evidence for a ~10% AGN-accretion-spin-up subpopulation at a₁ ≈ 0.9, decisively disfavoring the hierarchical-merger peak at 0.7 as the location of the high-spin component.
**Quality 7/10 · Relevance 6/10.** First population-level evidence for an AGN-accretion BBH subpopulation; reinterprets GW190521 as comparably consistent with AGN-disc spin-up rather than hierarchical merger. Caveats: theoretical priors do the work; 3-component is a strong choice; Bayes factor of 5.7 on a 166-event catalog has a track record of partial reversal.
[Full report](2026-05-12/07_Bartos_Haiman_AGN_BBH_spin_2605.09351)

## 🌌 Thematic Trends This Week

**The dominant theme is the maturation of the EMRI / Kerr-perturbation toolkit.** Three of the five top survivors live on this axis: Xu et al.'s eccentric scalar-cloud EMRIs find a *new* strong-field resonance channel that has no Newtonian counterpart, Chen-Cao-Jing claim a 3–10× speedup over pybhpt for generic-Kerr Teukolsky fluxes via a unified HeunC framework, and Pereñíguez gives a structural unification of the Regge-Wheeler-Zerilli and Bardeen-Press-Teukolsky formalisms with manifest axial-polar isospectrality. Different layers of the same stack: physical effects (Xu et al.), numerical algorithms (Chen-Cao-Jing), structural formalism (Pereñíguez). The fact that HeunC-based Kerr-Green-function technology surfaced *twice* this week — also in Motohashi & Suichi (2605.01964, just outside the window) — suggests this is the new conceptual setting for the Teukolsky connection problem.

**The Czech / Lukes-Gerakopoulos spinning-particle line continues incremental progress.** Shahzadi-Kološ-Zelenka-Lukes-Gerakopoulos add charge to the spinning particle in Schwarzschild + Wald, deploying 4D Poincaré sections + recurrence analysis on the now 3-DOF off-equatorial dynamics. This is the same methodological direction Vojtěch has co-developed; the natural next move is Kerr + a more realistic plasma field.

**On the LISA-population side**, Chen-Chandramouli-Pozzoli-Buscicchio-Barausse's SGWB-from-eccentric-sBBHs paper and Bartos-Haiman's AGN-accretion spin-up subpopulation analysis are doing the careful Bayesian work that the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA → LISA bridge requires, with attention to environments (dynamical friction, AGN accretion). Neither is a transformational result, but both are calibrating what is/isn't recoverable from realistic population data — useful for Vojtěch's environment-on-inspirals interests.

**Outside those threads**, Kastha-Vretinaris-Pook-Kolb-Krishnan's cusp-formation paper continues the quasi-local-horizon program in head-on non-spinning configurations, structurally interesting but not yet observationally connected.
