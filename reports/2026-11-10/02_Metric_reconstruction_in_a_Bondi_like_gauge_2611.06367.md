# 🌀 Metric reconstruction in a Bondi-like gauge

🌠 **Should-Read** · 🔬 Quality 6/10 · 🎯 Relevance 7/10

📎 **Citation:** Marc Casals, Stefan Hollands, Vahid Toomani, Peter Zimmerman, *Metric reconstruction in a Bondi-like gauge*, arXiv:[2611.06367](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06367) [gr-qc], submitted 04 Nov 2026.

> 💡 A practical recipe, with open-source code, turns a recent existence proof into an algorithm that rebuilds the metric disturbance caused by a small body orbiting a spinning black hole in a form that falls off properly far from the source, so radiated energy, momentum and wave content can be read off directly.

## 🔍 Executive Summary

Metric perturbations reconstructed from a Hertz potential in ingoing radiation gauge grow with radius at future null infinity. Hollands and Toomani proved that a residual gauge transformation plus a specific choice of the Hertz potential's undetermined radial integration 'constants' removes this growth; this paper shows the whole completion is generated by one scalar on null infinity (the 'Held Master Scalar'), obtained from the leading 1/r^5 coefficient of the spin+2 Weyl scalar by an angular Teukolsky-Starobinsky inversion, and gives explicit formulas for the four Hertz-potential constants and the gauge vector. In Schwarzschild everything is algebraic and diagonal in (l,m) modes; in Kerr it is tridiagonal in a spherical-harmonic basis, or done by spectral collocation in theta in an m-mode scheme. A proof-of-concept for one circular equatorial orbit (r0=10M, a=0 and a=0.667M) in a first-order puncture scheme shows the growing pieces cancelling to give 1/r fall-off, reproduces the known Detweiler redshift, and shows example Bondi mass, news and angular-momentum-aspect data in Kerr.

## 📣 Claimed Contribution

Turning the Hollands-Toomani existence theorem for an asymptotically flat (Bondi-like) gauge within the Green-Hollands-Zimmerman reconstruction framework into a practical algorithm: identifying a single Held Master Scalar that generates both the Hertz-potential completion pieces and the residual gauge vector, giving closed-form mode formulas in Schwarzschild and a tridiagonal scheme in Kerr, and a numerical implementation (including the corrector) in both (l,m) and m-mode form, with Bondi news, mass and angular-momentum aspects read off directly.

## ✅ Strengths

- Clean structural result: the entire completion (four Hertz-potential integration constants plus three residual-gauge-vector components) follows from one spin-weight-2 scalar on null infinity by explicit differential operators; in modes, its coefficients are just the Teukolsky-Starobinsky inversion of the asymptotic Weyl-scalar amplitudes.
- Fully closed-form mode formulas in Schwarzschild (diagonal in l,m); in Kerr the operators are banded/tridiagonal, so the extra cost over a standard radiation-gauge reconstruction is small and needs only the asymptotic Teukolsky amplitudes that production codes already output.
- Independent numerical cross-checks: the m-mode spectral implementation agrees with the closed-form (l,m) reference to about 1e-6 to 1e-7, fitted large-radius slopes are -1.000 to -1.003, and the redshift agrees with a standard radiation-gauge reconstruction (CCK) to within 4.7e-8.
- Open-source code (GHZ_numeric on GitHub) with a section-by-section code map.
- Scheme is independent of orbit type and perturbative order once an effective source is available; an appendix sketches punctures for generic orbits.

## ⚠️ Weaknesses

- Novelty is incremental relative to Hollands and Toomani (arXiv:2405.18604): the theorem and its proof, including the construction of the gauge vector and the completion, are already there; the new content is the repackaging into a single generating scalar, explicit mode formulas, and an implementation.
- Validation is thin and partly circular. The cancellation of growing terms at 'double precision' tests that the code implements formulas that cancel analytically by construction. The Detweiler redshift is by construction insensitive to the helically symmetric gauge vector, and the paper itself shows that piece vanishes, so the redshift agreement checks the existing reconstruction, not the new completion.
- Single orbit (r0=10M), single nonzero spin (a=0.667M), l up to 20, 21 angular collocation nodes, Weyl-scalar data obtained by fitting. No convergence study in Kerr and no error budget on the Bondi quantities, which are described as 'exemplary results'.
- Easy physical checks that were not done: the Bondi news extracted in Kerr is not compared against standard Teukolsky energy and angular-momentum fluxes, and the mass and angular-momentum aspects are not checked against flux-balance laws.
- The physical assumptions (that some gauge exists with Bondi fall-off, early-time decay, and Price-law late-time decay) are explicitly not proven for an eternal point-particle source; a possible log(r)/r^2 term is ruled out only from one plot.
- No comparison with simultaneous competing work that delivers the same practical product: Leather et al. (arXiv:2611.03888) provide first-order Kerr circular-orbit perturbations in near Bondi-Sachs gauge at large radius across spins -0.9 to 0.9 and radii out to 50M, and Spiers-Pound-Moxon (arXiv:2606.24816) give a general Bondi-Sachs/BMS-frame-fixing framework on Kerr.
- No conclusions or discussion section: the paper ends with the numerical results and appendices, so there is no assessment of limitations, cost, or the path to second order. There are minor drafting slips (for example 'a some Teukolsky solver'), and commented-out draft material remains in the source.

## 🤔 Skeptic's Cross-Examination

The headline numerical evidence is self-confirming: the growing terms cancel to machine precision because the formulas were derived to make them cancel, and the redshift is invariant under the gauge vector by construction. The only quantities that actually test the physical content of the new gauge, the Bondi news, mass aspect and angular-momentum aspect in Kerr, are shown as plots for one mode at one angle and are never compared to independently known fluxes. Meanwhile, a competing group has posted a broader, production-grade Kerr result with Bondi-type asymptotics the same week.

## 🆕 Novelty in Context

The paper is honest that its scheme is 'extracted from' Hollands-Toomani (2405.18604, Thm. 4 and App. J); the new piece is noticing that one scalar generates the whole completion, plus explicit mode formulas and a numerical demonstration. The literature check shows the practical goal, a self-force metric perturbation in a Bondi-type gauge in Kerr, is being pursued in parallel. Leather et al. (2609.08995) already transform to Bondi-Sachs gauge in second-order Teukolsky calculations in Schwarzschild. Spiers, Pound and Moxon (2606.24816) give a general iterative Bondi-Sachs and BMS-frame-fixing framework on Kerr. Leather et al. (2611.03888, posted two days earlier) deliver production-grade first-order Kerr circular-orbit perturbations in near Bondi-Sachs gauge at large radius over a wide parameter range. This paper cites the first two only in passing ('see ... for recent works') and cites 2611.03888 only for punctures. Its genuine distinctive feature is staying inside the ingoing-radiation-gauge/GHZ framework with an algebraic, mode-local completion. That is a cleaner construction, not a first.

## 🎯 Relevance to Your Research

Directly relevant to anyone building first- or second-order self-force pipelines from Teukolsky/radiation-gauge reconstruction: asymptotic flatness of the first-order field is what makes the second-order source manageable and makes BMS charges, news and memory readable. The practical value is that the fix needs only the asymptotic spin+2 Teukolsky amplitudes plus algebraic or banded angular operators. Read it alongside Hollands-Toomani 2405.18604 (the theorem), Spiers-Pound-Moxon 2606.24816, and Leather et al. 2611.03888 (the competing route to Bondi-type gauge in Kerr).

📖 **Where to start:** Sec. 3 (the four-step algorithm and the Held Master Scalar definition, Eqs. for the completion pieces and the gauge vector) and Sec. 4 (closed-form Schwarzschild mode formulas) carry the content. Sec. 5.6 has all the numerical evidence. Sec. 5.7 covers the Kerr (l,m) tridiagonal scheme if you plan to implement. The appendices on generic-orbit punctures can be skipped.

## 🚧 Caveats

- This is an implementation of the 2024 Hollands-Toomani theorem, not a new existence result.
- Proof of concept only: one circular orbit at r0=10M, one Kerr spin (a=0.667M), l up to 20.
- The redshift check cannot see the new gauge piece; the real test is the large-radius fall-off plots.
- The Kerr Bondi news and aspects are not checked against known fluxes or balance laws.
- Asymptotic flatness of the self-force field with an eternal source is assumed, not proven.
- No conclusions section and no comparison with Leather et al. 2611.03888 or Spiers-Pound-Moxon 2606.24816.
