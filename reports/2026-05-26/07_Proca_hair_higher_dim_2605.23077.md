# 🪄 Proca-type Hair of Rotating Black Holes in Higher Dimensions

**arxiv:** [2605.23077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23077)
**Authors:** David Kubizňák, Robert B. Mann, Marek Milička
**Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc

## TL;DR
A specifically tuned Proca theory (ℒ = −¼F² + R_ab A^a A^b) admits **any Killing vector** of any background as an exact stealth solution (vanishing T_ab). This promotes the classical Wald–Papapetrou "Killing → test Maxwell field" trick from a test-field statement in Ricci-flat backgrounds to an **exact, non-linear** solution with arbitrary amplitude on any background. Generalizes to conformal Killing vectors and (in a forthcoming paper) Killing–Yano tensors. Demonstrated by constructing exact charged/magnetized Myers–Perry hair in all d.

## Summary
Key identity: for a Killing vector ξ, the integrability condition ∇²ξ^a = −R^a_b ξ^b is precisely the EOM of the β=1 Proca-type theory (eq. 6). The non-trivial work is showing T_ab = 0 — the Ricci-coupling piece T̂_A exactly cancels T_EM (eqs. 15–18) when A = ξ, *using the second-derivative Killing identity*. The cancellation is **not** automatic from the EOM; it requires the full integrability machinery. The Lagrangian itself also vanishes on Killing fields (up to boundary, eq. 21). Stueckelberg promotion to a gauge theory + conserved current is given in Appendix A; conformal-Killing extension in Appendix B.

## Strengths
- 🧩 **The cancellation is non-trivial.** Unlike f(R) Kerr–Newman where g=2 is forced by metric form, here the stealthiness demands the *Killing second-derivative identity* (eq. 13) — a real consequence of hidden structure, not a tautology of the ansatz.
- The fine-tuned coupling β=1 is *derived*, not assumed; β=1 is the value at which the EOM coincides with the contracted Killing integrability condition. This is a structural observation.
- Genuine new exact solutions: the higher-d Myers–Perry stealth-charged/magnetized family is an exact (not test-field) solution with arbitrary Q, B_i — previously only known to linear order in field strength.
- Conformal-Killing extension (eq. 32) requires *adding* the (∇·A)² term with coefficient (d−1)/d — another structurally constrained, non-trivial result.
- The forthcoming Killing–Yano extension [36] would be the genuinely interesting payoff for Axis 2.

## Weaknesses / Skeptic's Attack
- 🌀 **"Hair" is misleading.** As the authors admit (Sec. IV), the field carries flux at infinity but does not modify thermodynamics; in the 4D Schwarzschild test case (per [39]), it is invisible to the first law. This is not Bekenstein-style hair distinguishing solutions with the same M, J — it's a co-existing stealth structure. Heusler's no-hair theorems are sidestepped by *changing the theory* (adding RA² coupling) rather than evading them within Einstein–Maxwell.
- The construction is genuinely *kinematic*: A_a = ξ_a means the vector field is geometrically determined by the metric's symmetries. There is no propagating Proca degree of freedom carrying independent data — it's the Killing vector wearing a Proca costume.
- Gauge invariance is broken (the action only has gauge symmetry after Stueckelberg; under A → A + dΛ the stealthiness *fails* — see remark after eq. 21). For a physical vector hair this is awkward.
- The Killing–Yano generalization is *promised* but deferred to [36], so the most interesting hidden-symmetry claim is not yet delivered in this paper.
- "Arbitrarily strong field with vanishing backreaction" is exactly what stealth means by definition; calling this "exact hair" oversells what is structurally a decorated vacuum.

## Relevance to Vojtěch's Research
**This is borderline.** Compared to the f(R) Kerr–Newman exclusion (2604.14066), this paper is **better**: the stealth cancellation is not guaranteed by metric form alone, and the fine-tuning β=1 is structurally determined. However, in the *current* paper the only hidden symmetry actually exploited is the ordinary Killing vector — the Killing–Yano content is announced for a follow-up. So for Vojtěch:
- The construction with **plain Killing vectors** is the higher-d generalization of Wald 1974, dressed up as exact solutions. Mildly novel.
- The **conformal Killing** extension (eq. 32, App. B) is more interesting — it pins down a specific Lagrangian via the conformal-Killing integrability identity.
- The **Killing–Yano case [36]** is what would really matter for Axis 2 (hidden symmetries → physical fields). That is the paper Vojtěch should watch — this is the appetizer.
- Connection to Kerr-NUT-AdS hidden symmetries: ref. [35] (Frolov–Krtouš–Kubizňák weakly charged Kerr–NUT–AdS) is exactly the kind of work this extends to non-perturbative amplitude.
