## This week's deeper re-evaluation: 6/10 · Should-Read

After a full read, the assessment holds at the lower end of last week's provisional range. The novelty headline is the combination of Dyson+ 2024 relativistic aerodynamics (drag + Magnus + lift) with Drummond's KerrSpinningFluxes + FEW + pybhpt for a flux-driven spinning-secondary EMRI inspiral. Concretely:

- The *spin-environment coupling itself* is parametrically tiny: `s·f_{S-EE}/f_{EE} ∼ 10⁻⁵` and `s·f_{LISS}/f_{GSF} ∼ 10⁻⁷`. The "curveball" (Magnus) physics in the title is sub-leading by 5–7 orders of magnitude.
- Drag dominates: `|Ċ_EE/Ċ_GW| ∼ 10⁷–10¹¹·ε`, so for ρ_g ~ 10⁻⁶ g/cm³ (ε ~ 10⁻¹¹) drag rivals 0PA GSF.
- Vacuum Fisher: Δχ ≈ ±0.42 (saturating prior); with EEs Δχ ≈ ±0.26 — a genuine but small degeneracy-breaking.
- Lindblom mismatch crossed within 4 yr LISA observation for both near-LSO and far-from-LSO orbits.

**Honest reassessment:** The actual physics result is "drag is huge in dense discs and aerodynamic *geometry* partially disentangles χ from β" — useful but incremental given Speri/Gair, Kocsis/Yunes/Loeb, and Dyson et al. already paved the road. Constant gas density, equatorial-only, Fisher-only. The title oversells.
