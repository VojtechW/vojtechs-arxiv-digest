# ☄️ Bandopadhyay, Amend, Coughlin, Nixon, Pasham, Wevers — The Role of Stellar Spin in Repeating Partial Tidal Disruption Events

**arXiv:** [2606.02692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02692)
**Categories:** astro-ph.HE
**Length:** 9 pages, 5 figures, 1 appendix; submitted
**Recommendation:** Worth-Skimming · **Quality:** 6/10
**Collaborator flag:** ⭐ Tier-1 — Dheeraj R. Pasham

## Summary

Hydrodynamical SPH simulations (phantom + MESA progenitors, 10⁶ particles) show that adding a substantial prograde stellar spin (λ ~ 0.7–0.8, Ω ~ Ω_p) lets a high-mass main-sequence star reproduce the observed progressively dimming flares of several rpTDE candidates over more than two encounters — closing a tension where prior simulations couldn't get >2 successively dimmer flares without destroying the star. Interpreted as "strong indirect evidence" for a Hills-capture origin (tidally locked tight binary supplying near-Ω_* spin).

## Key Results

- Existing rpTDE simulations cannot reproduce >2 successively dimmer flares without disruption; adding initial spin fixes this.
- For prograde Ω ~ Ω_p the BH cannot torque the star up much further, so each encounter produces less mass loss and dimmer fallback peaks.
- Demonstrated for 1 M⊙ ZAMS (β = 0.6), 3 M⊙ MAMS and TAMS (β = 0.6, 1.0).
- Hills capture from a tidally locked tight binary naturally supplies the required initial rotation; therefore the dimming-flare phenomenon is interpreted as strong indirect evidence for the Hills channel.
- AT2018fyk (factor-of-10 dimming) is acknowledged as not naturally fit; relativistic/chaotic three-body pericenter changes are invoked.

## Strengths

- Clean mechanism: identifies a specific missing ingredient (initial spin) and tests it directly.
- Builds on a mature methodology (phantom SPH + MESA) from a productive group.
- Honest about limitations (AT2018fyk, retrograde regime).
- Appendix on relaxation procedure for rotating-star equilibria with stability checks.

## Weaknesses

- Incremental: extends Bandopadhyay 2024 by turning on initial spin. The underlying physical insight (spin reduces the effective tidal radius via Golightly 2019 Eq. 6) is pre-existing.
- Only ~4 simulations shown — parameter dependence on (λ, β, M_*) is asserted rather than mapped.
- Factor-of-2 dimming reproduces eRASSt-J045650, AT2022dbl, AT2021aeuk, but the headline AT2018fyk requires hand-waving about chaotic 3-body relativistic effects.
- "Strong indirect evidence for Hills" overclaims: prograde tidally locked spin is one consistent scenario, but tidal-heating inflation or other channels can also produce dimming. The paper does not falsify alternatives.
- No quantitative discussion of Kozai/secular channels vs Hills for producing the inferred spin signature.

## Honest Assessment

A solid but narrow follow-up paper from a productive group: identifies a real tension, proposes a plausible fix, demonstrates feasibility. The Hills-capture overinterpretation and the small parameter scan are the main issues. For Vojtěch the Pasham coauthorship plus the rpTDE/Hills/QPE connection make it worth a quick skim, but it is not field-changing.
