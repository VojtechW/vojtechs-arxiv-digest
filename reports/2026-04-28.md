## Reading Priority

### Should-Read

**🌀 Astrophysically realistic secondary spins trigger chaos in Schwarzschild** — A claim that EMRI chaos persists in the realistic spin range, with a frequency-domain "spectral flatness" diagnostic; reopens the chaos-vs-adiabaticity question for spinning EMRI secondaries.

- [2604.20533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20533) — Yuan, Jiao, Lei, Shi, Lai, Shao, Tian
- Quality: 5/10 | Relevance: 8/10
- Topically central — spinning particles in Schwarzschild, chaos, GW signatures — but the methodology is thin: no Lyapunov / rotation-number diagnostic is reported, the (J, S) regime is not mapped against the Suzuki–Maeda B2 boundary, and there is no flux comparison or SNR analysis. The "100×" spectral-flatness ratio is not yet detector-physics.
- *Caveat:* Spectral flatness is not necessarily a clean chaos diagnostic — adiabatic frequency drift and finite-window leakage can mimic it. Independent verification needed before citing the conclusions.
- Adjacent to the Lukes-Gerakopoulos / Witzany 2021 chaos-in-EMRI review.
- [Full report](2026-04-21/01_Yuan_et_al_spinning_chaos_Schwarzschild_2604.20533)

**🪄 Regularized Teukolsky Green function in Schwarzschild** — Closed-form Hadamard parametrix for the spin-2 Teukolsky equation on a conformally-product (ℳ₂ × 𝕊²) background, with explicit angular factor and a clean retarded-minus-direct ("tail") representation.

- [2604.21219](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21219) — Aruquipa, Casals, Nolan
- Quality: 7/10 | Relevance: 8/10
- Solid analytic Hadamard work by the Casals group; provides the kind of structural factorization that mode-sum / puncture self-force codes need as input. Restricted to Schwarzschild and constant-radius worldlines, with no Kerr extension yet.
- *Caveat:* The whole point of EMRI is Kerr; the paper does not sketch how the conformal-product trick survives off Schwarzschild.
- [Full report](2026-04-21/02_Aruquipa_Casals_Nolan_Teukolsky_GreenFn_2604.21219)

**💥 GRRMHD state transitions in non-jetted TDEs** — Late-stage sub-Eddington TDE tori become thermally unstable on tens-of-days timescales, producing soft-X-ray excess followed by ~2 dex luminosity drop; consistent with AT2021ehb's late-time spectral evolution.

- [2604.23916](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23916) — Curd, Heridia, Ahiyya, Anantua
- Quality: 7/10 | Relevance: 8/10
- Connects the Cooling Envelope Model phase of TDEs to a real X-ray observable, with a primary-spin scan (a∈[0, 0.9]). Useful background context for the QPE-as-EMRI scenarios that overlap with the Pasham/Suková track. Single (M⋆, M_BH) point and inherited CEM initial conditions are the main caveats.
- *Caveat:* No outflow/wind analysis; radiation-transport approximations not benchmarked against full Compton transport.
- [Full report](2026-04-21/03_Curd_et_al_GRRMHD_TDE_state_transitions_2604.23916)

**🎵 Dissipative tides at 1PN in spinning compact binaries** — Spin-induced tidal dissipation enters the GW phase at 2.5 PN order with a logarithmic frequency dependence, and the EMRI limit recovers known horizon absorption fluxes.

- [2604.21990](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21990) — Balivada, Hegade K. R., Yunes
- Quality: 6.5/10 | Relevance: 7/10
- The 2.5 PN ln(f) phase term is a clean, observably non-degenerate-with-coalescence-phase signature for spin-aligned binaries. Recovers BHPT horizon flux in q→∞ as a sanity check. Identifies a possibly missing redshift correction in some recent worldline-EFT calculations — that claim is provocative and deserves an explicit equation-by-equation comparison.
- *Caveat:* Quasi-circular and spin-aligned only; no Fisher-matrix detectability forecast.
- Notable-author flag: Yunes.
- [Full report](2026-04-21/04_Balivada_Hegade_Yunes_dissipative_tides_1PN_2604.21990)

### Worth-Skimming

**🧩 Kähler decoupling for Kerr perturbations** — A hidden-Kähler-structure reformulation of why Teukolsky's equations decouple, both for spin-2 (similarity transform of a Kähler-Laplace operator) and spin-1 (conformal invariance of Maxwell on a Kähler background).

- [2604.22424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22424) — Green, Krasnov, Shaw
- Quality: 6/10 | Relevance: 7/10
- Sits squarely on Vojtěch's "non-trivial integrable structure" axis — but only earns the value if it is more than a relabeling of the type-D / GHP story. The Lorentzian Kähler structure is *complex*, which softens the geometric punchline. No new master equation, no new mode content, no new conserved currents are claimed; the natural test is whether the Kähler framework cleanly extends to other type-D backgrounds (Plebański–Demiański, NUT) where ordinary GHP is more painful.
- *Caveat:* Risk of being a structural relabeling of Petrov-D structure. Cf. the f(R) Kerr-Newman anti-example (2604.14066).
- Notable-author flag: Stephen R. Green.
- [Full report](2026-04-21/05_Green_Krasnov_Shaw_Kahler_decoupling_2604.22424)

**🌐 Black-hole response theory and its exact shockwave limit** — Plefka-group WQFT response-function organization of the gravitational self-force expansion; in the Aichelburg-Sexl shockwave limit the full PM series resums into a Weinberg-phase-dressed Born amplitude.

- [2604.22009](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22009) — Bohnenblust, Eriksen, Hoogeveen, Jakobsen, Plefka
- Quality: 7/10 | Relevance: 6/10
- Useful dictionary between WQFT/amplitudes machinery and the Detweiler / MiSaTaQuWa SF expansion; the all-orders shockwave resummation is a hep-th showpiece. But the application is *probe-on-fixed-background*, not actual SF back-reaction, and no EMRI-relevant numbers are delivered.
- *Caveat:* Beware the "exact in G" framing — it means PM-resummed in a fixed shockwave background, not self-consistent SF. Track the Plefka group's follow-ups for actual 1SF observables.
- [Full report](2026-04-21/06_Bohnenblust_et_al_BH_response_shockwave_2604.22009)

## Papers That Failed Critical Review

1. **2604.24413** — Meng, Zhang, Yang — "Gravitational waves of EMRIs in a rotating black hole with Dehnen dark-matter halo"
   - FAIL — Template paper. Standard Teukolsky/Sasaki-Nakamura pipeline applied to a Dehnen-dressed Kerr metric. No new methodology, no separability proof, no astrophysical motivation for the specific halo choice; the "detectability" criterion lives at fine-tuned parameters orders of magnitude beyond any realistic galactic profile. This is exactly the genre Vojtěch's preferences explicitly flag as noise.

2. **2604.22734** — Bishoyi, Field, Lau — "Radiation outer boundary conditions and near-to-far field signal transformations for the Bardeen–Press equation"
   - FAIL on relevance — Technically rigorous (exact-kernel BCs with explicit error bounds for exponential-sum approximations), but restricted to the Bardeen–Press (a=0) case, while the EMRI community has largely moved to hyperboloidal foliations on Kerr (Panosso Macedo, Ansorg, Markakis). Field's own group has parallel hyperboloidal-DG work that supersedes this approach for production EMRI tooling.

3. **2604.21522** — Bini, Damour, Geralico — "Quadrupolar bremsstrahlung waveform at 3.5 PN accuracy"
   - FAIL on relevance (not on quality) — Solid +0.5 PN extension of the Bini–Damour–Geralico MPM scattering programme, with a clean BMS-frame consistency check between MPM and EFT. But scope is hyperbolic/scattering, quadrupole-only, non-spinning, and provides no Kerr / bound-orbit / self-force content. Real-but-narrow contribution to the PM-amplitudes ecosystem.

4. **2604.22021** — Tommasini, Vu, Scheel, Teukolsky — "Data-driven acceleration of eccentricity reduction for binary black hole simulations"
   - FAIL on novelty — A Gaussian-process-regression accelerator on top of the standard SpEC eccentricity-reduction iteration. ~4× iteration reduction is a real production benefit but the paper is a tooling note rather than a research contribution; needs a clearer comparison to the [Knapp–Chatziioannou–Pfeiffer–Scheel–Kidder 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02997) parameter-control loop and a discussion of generalization across the (q, χ, e) training domain.

5. **2604.20971** — Garg, Mayer, Wu, Ali-Haïmoud, Lin — "Chaotic migration of LISA EMRIs in a turbulent accretion disk"
   - FAIL — Topically core (turbulent disk torques on EMRIs, GW dephasing) but the implementation is preliminary: turbulent torques are modelled as a phenomenological zero-mean Gaussian fluctuation around the laminar value (with a normalization C borrowed from a hydrodynamic — not MHD — simulation), no actual MHD validation is provided, no chaos diagnostic (Lyapunov / rotation number / Poincaré section) is shown, and the comparison to prior laminar-disk dephasing literature is not laid out side-by-side. The "chaotic migration" headline is not yet supported by the dynamical evidence presented; the authors themselves position the paper as motivation for follow-up MHD work.

## Thematic Trends This Week

The week's centre of gravity sits in a familiar place: post-Newtonian / post-Minkowskian extensions on one side (Bini–Damour bremsstrahlung; Yunes-group spin-induced dissipative tides; Plefka WQFT response theory), and Schwarzschild-restricted analytic infrastructure on the other (Casals-group Hadamard Teukolsky Green function; Field–Lau Bardeen–Press boundary conditions; Green–Krasnov–Shaw Kähler decoupling). What is conspicuously thin is the *Kerr* side — almost nothing new on Kerr separability, hidden symmetries, or generic (Carter-constant-broken) spinning-secondary dynamics in the genuinely Kerr regime.

A second thread is the resurgence of EMRI-environment work, partly driven by the cooling-envelope-model TDE programme. The Curd et al. GRRMHD state-transition simulations and the Garg et al. turbulent-disk dephasing study both probe the late-stage sub-Eddington disk regime that the QPE-as-EMRI scenarios live in — although only the former achieves a genuine MHD-grade calculation; the latter remains motivational. Together they suggest the next round of useful work in this niche will require *MHD turbulence drag torques on an EMRI secondary*, computed self-consistently rather than via a Gaussian fluctuation ansatz.

Third — and almost a constant feature of the gr-qc arXiv now — is the steady stream of "compute X for dressed/exotic background Y" papers (Dehnen-halo EMRIs, Bumblebee-plasma shadows, Kerr-EMDA greybody, Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet thin disks). They are noise, but they are abundant noise; ignoring them quickly is itself a useful filter.
