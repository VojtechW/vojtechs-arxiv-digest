# 🌀 QPEs from Warped Disk Collisions with EMRIs

**arXiv:** [2605.24905](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24905)
**Authors:** Bo-An Chen, Bei You, Giovanni Miniutti, Ning Jiang, Zhen Pan, Tao Yang, Xi-Long Fan, et al.
**Categories:** astro-ph.HE astro-ph.CO
**Quality:** 6/10 — **Relevance:** High — **Tier:** Should-Read

## Summary

Proposes that QPE correlated long/short–strong/weak patterns arise from EMRI collisions with a **Bardeen-Petterson warped** TDE disk. Introduces a "Brightness-Recurrence Diagram" (BRD) to encode SMBH spin sign and orbit type. Applied to GSN 069 with a LISA follow-up sketch.

## Key claims

- A radial twist (~12°) and inclination variation (~10%) between pericenter (154 M) and apocenter naturally produce ~15% recurrence modulation and ~20% energy modulation — matching observed correlations in GSN 069.
- The sign of the BRD ellipse tilt and its rotation direction encode (i) prograde vs retrograde EMRI orientation w.r.t. SMBH spin, and (ii) star vs stellar-mass-BH secondary.
- GSN 069 data favor either a prograde star or retrograde 20 M☉ BH at A ≈ 160 M, e ≈ 0.01.
- FastEMRIWaveforms used to argue LISA could distinguish a = ±0.9 with mismatch ~0.99 over 4 yr if the system decays to A ~ 20 M.

## Strengths

- Identifies a real observational tension: the systematic burst-energy/recurrence correlation is not naturally produced by flat or rigidly-precessing-disk collision models.
- The warp mechanism is physically plausible (Bardeen-Petterson is unavoidable for thin tilted TDE disks) and uses standard Pringle/Ogilvie warp formalism.
- BRD as a diagnostic is new, simple, and falsifiable; preliminary comments on eRO-QPE1/2 and RX J1301.9+2747 give discriminating power.
- Quantitative match to GSN 069 light curve with 3.5PN trajectory integration; omitted physics clearly stated.

## Weaknesses

- Self-consistency gap: EMRI assumed equatorial in SMBH frame while the disk is warped — but nodal precession of a tilted EMRI in a warped disk would significantly complicate the geometry, and this is acknowledged-but-ignored.
- Eccentricity reduced from e ~ 0.04 (flat-disk inference) to e ~ 0.01 to make warp dominate — looks like tuning.
- Statistical significance of the BRD ellipse fit is not given; Chandra error bars are large.
- Crude collision-energy model (E ∝ v_rel^±2 with single Coulomb log); radiative transfer linking collision energy to X-ray light curve is sidestepped.
- BRD sign depends on twist phase lead/lag — sensitive to α, H/R, surface density profile — robustness not explored.
- LISA follow-up assumes hypothetical decay from A = 160 M to A = 20 M without deriving it from the disk torques central to the rest of the paper.
- Σ ∝ r^{−3/5} chosen without justification.

## Relevance to Vojtěch

High. Direct EMRI + QPE axis, BH spin, disk warps, multimessenger LISA strategy. The BRD construction is a simple idea worth knowing about — and worth critiquing technically (especially the equatorial-EMRI-in-warped-disk inconsistency and the unjustified orbital-decay assumption for LISA).

## Verdict

Should-Read. Useful conceptual addition to the QPE star-disk literature but executed loosely.
