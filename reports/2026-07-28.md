## Reading Priority

### 🌟 Must-Read

**🕳️ Probing an Intermediate-Mass Black Hole Companion of Sgr A* with Pulsar Timing**
Zexin Hu, Lijing Shao — [arXiv:2607.24201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24201) — astro-ph.HE
A full three-body 1PN pulsar-timing model (Newtonian + 1PN + spin-orbit + the nonlinear three-body term) showing that one SKA-era pulsar orbiting Sgr A* would sharply constrain a hidden IMBH companion: Shapiro-delay double-period modulation ~1–10 ms, secular residuals ~150 ms/5 yr, and a 95% mass-exclusion curve occupying parameter space distinct from S-star astrometry. Directly extends the S301 / Galactic-center-spin thread of the last two digests.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 9/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* premise rests on a close GC pulsar that does not yet exist; noise thresholds imported from a companion paper; IMBH-vs-stellar-cusp degeneracy acknowledged but unsolved; 1PN only.
[Full report](2026-07-28/01_Hu_Shao_IMBH_companion_SgrA_pulsar_timing_2607.24201)

**🧩 Octupole Moments and the Non-Universality of Free-Fall in General Relativity**
Abraham I. Harte, Paul Ramond — [arXiv:2607.21314](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21314) — gr-qc
Carries the Dixon/MPD extended-body program to octupolar order: proves only the trace-free octupole affects motion in vacuum (40→24 components), finds this breaks at hexadecapole order, and shows octupoles produce torques *forbidden* at quadrupolar order in type-D (Schwarzschild/Kerr) backgrounds, plus hidden-momentum effects in the Newtonian limit. Rigorous index algebra, honest about its loose ends.
Quality 8/10 · Relevance 7/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* purely kinematic — equations of motion never solved, no observational prediction; effects are tiny (O(1/c²)); astrophysical framing is rhetorical.
**Collaborator:** 🚩 Abraham I. Harte (Tier 2). Co-author Ramond also drives the Ramond–Isoyama spinning-body series (see Updates).
[Full report](2026-07-28/02_Harte_Ramond_octupole_nonuniversality_freefall_2607.21314)

### 🌠 Should-Read

**🌌 Semi-Analytical Model for the Evolution of Stellar Binaries in the Empty Loss Cone**
McGuire, Grishin, Mandel, Levin — [arXiv:2607.18761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18761) — astro-ph.HE/GA
Cheap secular model (periapsis kicks + GR precession + tides + relaxation) for highly eccentric binaries around an SMBH, run over 3×10⁵ systems. GR precession quenches mergers: 84% (Newtonian) → 3% (GR) → 0.4% (GR + tides). Ships analytic fits for final orbital-element distributions as inputs for HVS/TDE/rate work; includes a genuine e·j orthogonality correction that removes a spurious drift seen in concurrent papers.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 8/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* headline merger fractions not robust — a full N-body study (Marklund+ 2025) gets 29–46%; tidal prescription uncertain by ×10; narrow regime β<0.15.
[Full report](2026-07-28/03_McGuire_Grishin_Mandel_Levin_empty_loss_cone_binaries_2607.18761)

**🎵 Schrödinger Perturbation Theory for Black Hole Quasinormal Modes**
Lestingi, Sberna, Green — [arXiv:2607.19492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19492) — gr-qc/hep-th
Lifts textbook perturbation theory to the non-self-adjoint QNM problem via the orthogonal symplectic-current bilinear form, giving an arbitrary-order frequency-shift formula for Kerr. The interesting result is negative: the discrete-QNM sum for the mode shift is shown to **diverge**, making basis incompleteness explicit. Second-order checks to ~14 digits on Pöschl–Teller and slow Kerr.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 6/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* the genuinely new spectral sum is exactly the part that diverges and is bypassed; continuum regulariser never evaluated; toy problems only, non-degenerate linear-source case.
**Collaborators:** 🚩 Laura Sberna (Tier 2), Stephen R. Green (Tier 3).
[Full report](2026-07-28/04_Lestingi_Sberna_Green_Schrodinger_QNM_PT_2607.19492)

**💫 Extreme Mass-Ratio Inspirals into Newtonian Proca Stars**
J. B. Silva, R. Brito — [arXiv:2607.18405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18405) — gr-qc/hep-ph
First calculation of energy + charge loss from a secondary inspiralling inside a self-gravitating vector-boson (Proca) star, via a vectorial Schrödinger–Poisson reduction and three cross-validated flux methods. Ground-state Proca and scalar boson stars lose energy within ~20% (same Newtonian energy density); hedgehog states lose 1–2 dex less. Opens a new environmental-EMRI object class.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 6/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* essentially a null result at Newtonian order — the distinguishing prolate morphology needs relativity they don't do; no waveform, dephasing, or LISA detectability; circular equatorial orbits only.
[Full report](2026-07-28/05_Silva_Brito_EMRI_Proca_stars_2607.18405)

### 🔦 Worth-Skimming

Arias, Kopčan, Kubizňák, Milička — [arXiv:2607.18871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18871) — gr-qc/hep-th
Mixed-symmetry generalized Killing tensors built by contracting the Kerr-NUT-(A)dS Killing–Yano tower, giving geodesic-parallel-transported objects plus a "conformal-like" null variant. Clean, honest — but the objects are guaranteed by the known KY structure and their trace just returns the standard Carter constants; no new scalar constant of motion.

## Papers That Failed Critical Review

- **Nonlinear electrodynamics in Kerr-Newman-NUT-Λ spacetime** ([2607.22977](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22977), Galindo-Uriarte, Bretón, Lämmerzahl, Macías). Technically sound but derivative: adds NUT charge and Λ to a pre-existing aligned NLED-Kerr generating scheme. Any separability/Killing–Yano structure is guaranteed by the Plebański-class form (the authors say so), the ring singularity survives, and matching the observed Λ forces a negligible coupling. Deep-read; no new integrability. (Quality 5, Relevance 2.)
- **Angular momentum flux through 1PN for noncircular EMD binaries** ([2607.17887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17887), Tan & Li). Correct and genuinely first-of-kind (the eccentric angular-momentum balance law in Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton), with good limit checks — but incremental, low-PN, and squarely in beyond-GR *comparable-mass* territory. The paper itself concedes astrophysical BHs carry negligible charge. Cut on relevance, not correctness. (Quality 6, Relevance 3.)
- **GWTC-5.0: Tests of General Relativity** ([2607.19293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19293), LVK Collaboration). Seven standard consistency/parametrized tests on the latest catalog with no reported GR deviation — the "predictable null result + routine catalog update" category, absent new methodology. The underlying GWTC-5.0 data release itself is worth a glance, but the tests-of-GR paper is not a critical read.
- **Template exotic-metric "GW signatures of periodic orbits" papers**: magnetized Ernst BH ([2607.24154](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24154)), Bonanno–Reuter regular BH ([2607.18627](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18627)), Schwarzschild-like DM-halo BH ([2607.24144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24144)). All follow the same "classify bound geodesics → plug into the Poincaré periodic-orbit taxonomy → produce a waveform" template on an ad hoc metric. Hard-excluded genre.
- **Torsionful Killing-Yano forms under T-duality** ([2607.18842](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18842), Kelekçi, Ertem, Açık). Formal string-theory transformation conditions for KY forms under Buscher rules; off-core and no gravitational-dynamics payoff.

## Thematic Trends This Week

The Galactic-center measurement thread that has dominated recent digests continued to be the strongest signal. Following the S301 discovery and the Rom–Sari cusp-dynamics ledger, this week added a concrete forecast (Hu & Shao) for using an SKA-era Sgr A* pulsar to hunt an IMBH companion, and a population model (McGuire et al.) for the eccentric-binary channel that feeds the Hills mechanism, hypervelocity stars and TDE/QPE progenitors. Together they reinforce a maturing picture in which precise timing/astrometry of a few special objects near Sgr A* — rather than population statistics alone — carries the discriminating power for the central potential and its companions.

On the formal side, two papers pushed the analytic structure of test-body and perturbation dynamics: Harte & Ramond's octupolar extension of Dixon multipole motion (with the pleasing result that higher multipoles unlock torques symmetry forbids at lower order) and the Nottingham group's arbitrary-order QNM perturbation theory, whose honest headline is that the natural QNM-sum representation diverges. Both are "structure-revealing" rather than phenomenological, which is exactly the Axis-2 territory worth tracking — in contrast to the recurring flood of "compute geodesics/QNMs/GW signatures of exotic-metric-X" preprints, three of which surfaced this week and were hard-excluded.

Exotic-compact-object EMRIs also reappeared: the Proca-star inspiral (Silva & Brito) extends the scalar-boson-star EMRI line into the vector sector, though its Newtonian punchline is a near-degeneracy with the scalar case. The lesson repeated across several of this week's near-misses is that the discriminating physics almost always lives at the relativistic / higher-multipole / eccentric level that the papers defer — a useful calibration point for which environmental-EMRI results will actually matter for LISA.
