# 💫 Extreme Mass-Ratio Inspirals into Newtonian Proca Stars

**Citation:** J. B. Silva & R. Brito, *Extreme mass-ratio inspirals into Newtonian Proca stars*, arXiv:[2607.18405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18405) [gr-qc, hep-ph], 20 Jul 2026 (CENTRA / IST Lisbon).

## Executive Summary
The first (Newtonian) calculation of energy loss from a point secondary inspiralling *inside* a self-gravitating vector-boson (Proca) star. Reducing Einstein–Proca to a vectorial Schrödinger–Poisson system, the authors build spherically symmetric ("hedgehog," excited) and axisymmetric ("prolate," ground-state) Newtonian Proca stars and compute energy and U(1)-charge fluxes for a secondary on circular equatorial orbits, with and without a central "parasitic" black hole. The headline: the ground-state Proca star and its scalar boson-star counterpart lose energy nearly identically (within ~20%), because at Newtonian order both share the same spherically symmetric energy density, while the hedgehog state loses 1–2 orders of magnitude less. Three independent numerical schemes cross-validate.

## Key Contributions
- First treatment of an EMRI into a self-gravitating vector soliton, opening a new "environment" object class for LISA-band dynamics.
- A vectorial Schrödinger–Poisson construction of Newtonian Proca-star profiles (hedgehog and prolate) with a central-BH option.
- Cross-validated flux computation (full fundamental-matrix, source-dominated, and high-frequency methods agree).
- Quantified comparison to scalar boson-star EMRIs, showing near-degeneracy of the ground states at Newtonian order.

## Strengths
- Careful, methodical, triple-cross-checked numerics; reproduces known limits (Adshead–Lozanov profiles, Herdeiro state ordering, scalar-star fluxes).
- Notably honest: states plainly that the GW flux is not computable in their Newtonian setup and that the physically distinguishing prolate morphology needs a relativistic treatment they do not perform.

## Weaknesses
- The headline is essentially a **null result**: the vector nature is invisible precisely because the Newtonian ground-state energy density matches the scalar case; the genuine discriminator (prolate shape) is deferred.
- No waveform, no dephasing, no SNR / LISA detectability, no degeneracy analysis — detectability is inherited from cited scalar-star work rather than demonstrated.
- Circular, equatorial orbits only; generic orbits merely conjectured. A formalism-plus-benchmark paper, not a phenomenological one.
