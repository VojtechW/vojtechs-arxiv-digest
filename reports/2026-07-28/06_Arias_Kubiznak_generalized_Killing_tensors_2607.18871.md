# 🔮 On Generalized (Conformal) Killing Tensors

**Citation:** Cynthia Arias, David Kopčan, David Kubizňák, Marek Milička, *On Generalized (Conformal) Killing Tensors*, arXiv:[2607.18871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18871) [gr-qc, hep-th], 21 Jul 2026 (Charles Univ., Prague).

## Executive Summary
The authors relax the Collinson–Howarth notion of a generalized Killing tensor (GKT) to drop index symmetry, define equivalence classes whose representatives are parallel-transported along geodesics, and construct explicit higher-rank, mixed-symmetry examples in Kerr-NUT-(A)dS by partially contracting the Killing–Yano tower. In D = 2n+ε they obtain (n−2+ε) irreducible "Riemann-type" objects from the principal-tensor tower, plus a "conformal-like" variant (CGKT) giving tensors parallel-transported along *null* geodesics. Derivations are honest index algebra; the trace of the new objects just returns the standard Carter Killing tensors.

## Key Contributions
- A cleaner definition of GKTs without imposed index symmetry, with equivalence classes tied to parallel transport.
- First non-trivial higher-dimensional examples of the long-dormant Collinson–Howarth construction, built explicitly from the Kerr-NUT-(A)dS Killing–Yano tower.
- A "conformal-like" generalization yielding null-geodesic parallel-transported tensors.

## Strengths
- Genuinely clean, checkable computations (parallel-transport proofs, an explicit vanishing-incompatibility argument, and the demonstrated failure of the naive "square" construction).
- Unusually candid self-assessment: labels the p = 2 case "reducible and trivial," and admits the "conformal" object has *no* actual conformal symmetry (hence "conformal-like").

## Weaknesses
- Close to the anti-pattern Vojtěch dislikes: the objects are **built by contraction from the already-known Killing–Yano tower**, so their existence is guaranteed by that structure, and their trace merely reproduces standard Carter constants. No new scalar constant of motion is produced — the yield is parallel-transported *tensors*, not new integrability.
- The headline "conformal" generalization is conceded to lose the defining conformal property; the name is aspirational.
- Novelty only appears for n ≥ 3 (large D); the separability role is explicitly left unproven.

## Novelty context
The Killing–Yano tower, principal tensor, and Kerr-NUT-(A)dS integrability are the Frolov–Krtouš–Kubizňák program (Living Rev. Rel. 20, 6). The base concept is Collinson & Howarth. Closely related recent work: Gray–Odak–Krtouš–Kubizňák (JHEP 07 (2025) 098, arXiv:2504.18287) on irreducible Killing tensors. So this is a real-but-modest extension/repackaging within an existing framework: the mixed-symmetry representatives are new, but structurally guaranteed rather than surprising.
