# 🎇 Twist-free axisymmetric gravitational waves near the BH threshold

**Citation:** A. Adhikari, T. Ledvinka, D. Cors, T. W. Baumgarte, A. Khirnov, B. Brügmann, D. Hilditch, *Comparing twist-free axisymmetric gravitational waves near the black hole threshold*, arXiv:[2607.10843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10843) [gr-qc], 12 Jul 2026.

## Executive Summary
A direct follow-up to Baumgarte et al. PRL 131, 181401 (2023) [arXiv:2305.17171], this paper extends the three-code (bamps / prague / sphGR) cross-comparison of critical vacuum-GW collapse to two previously-untreated families: off-center Brill waves (now in prague and sphGR) and Teukolsky-like time-asymmetric waves (now in bamps). Its purpose is confidence-building for the emerging consensus that the vacuum axisymmetric threshold has family-dependent critical exponents γ and echoing periods Δ — i.e. no unique DSS critical solution — up to the ~7-digit tuning currently achievable.

## Key Contributions
- First bamps evolution of the Ledvinka–Khirnov (2021) time-asymmetric initial data; recovers prague's γ ≈ 0.37 (far) / γ ≈ 0.51 (near) and Δ ≈ 1.1–1.3.
- Off-center Brill data reproduced across all three codes with excellent agreement in the invariant ζ along timelike geodesics between curvature peaks.
- Head-to-head double-null-coordinate reconstruction of best-tuned supercritical time-asymmetric spacetimes; bifurcated apparent horizons found in both bamps and prague (M_AH/M_ADM ≈ 2–5% at formation).
- Detailed comparison of two AH finders (shooting vs flow), including consistent detection of marginally *inner* trapped surfaces (MITS).
- Diagnostic identification of a coordinate-singularity window in bamps' default GH damped-wave gauge (𝒢₁), fixed by tuning the parameter p=1→0.5 (𝒢₂) — a concrete numerical-relativity lesson.
- Reinforces evidence that in the time-asymmetric family the successive curvature peaks do *not* accumulate at the origin (contrast with the A<0 Brill family), suggesting different threshold-solution geometry.

## Strengths
- Genuine three-code, four-family systematic comparison — rare in critical collapse.
- Coordinate-independent quantities (ζ on axis, double-null Kretschmann maps) make agreement claims meaningful.
- Honest documentation of failure modes.
- Nicely updates the historical Table 1 (Eppley 1978 → Baumgarte 2026).

## Weaknesses
- Not a discovery paper: strengthens rather than overturns the 2023 PRL conclusions.
- Tuning stalls at ~7 decimals — every conclusion remains hedged "at the current level of fine-tuning".
- No new theoretical model for why exponents nearly double in the "near" region.
- Post-processing tools for identifying DSS from an arbitrary geodesic remain unfinished.
