# 🚀 An off-axis relativistic jet in a dwarf galaxy

**Citation:** D. Levine, G. Hallinan, J. J. Somalwar, D. Z. Dong, E. Nakar, K. Hotokezaka, V. Ravi, A. Horesh, J. M. Miller, C. Law, S. T. Myers, S. K. Ocker, D. D. Kelso et al., *A spectacular multi-wavelength transient associated with an off-axis relativistic jet*, arXiv:[2607.10047](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10047) [astro-ph.HE], 11 Jul 2026.

## Executive Summary
The authors present **AT 2019ijn / VT J131240+211339**, a VLASS-discovered radio transient with a coincident luminous optical flare (νL_ν,opt ≈ 7.8×10⁴³ erg/s, M_g = −21.1) in a **star-forming dwarf galaxy at z = 0.273** (M_* ≈ 9×10⁸ M_☉). The radio emission turned on ~1 year after the optical peak, remained bright for >6 years, and carries E ≈ 2×10⁵² erg. Modeling of the flat radio spectrum favours a **narrow (θ_j ≈ 0.1 rad), off-axis (θ_obs ≈ 0.7–1 rad) relativistic jet** decelerating in a wind-like medium. The favoured interpretation is a **jetted TDE from an IMBH (M_BH ∼ 10⁴–10⁶ M_☉)** — an off-axis analogue of AT 2022cmc at 4× lower redshift.

## Key Contributions
1. Robust chance-association (p ≈ 2.3×10⁻⁸) establishing radio–optical coincidence.
2. First off-axis jetted TDE candidate with the delayed radio counterpart caught during on-going deceleration, enabling multi-epoch spectral fits (998 d and 1997 d rest-frame).
3. Careful elimination of LGRB, SLSNe, magnetar, and BH–stellar merger channels using energetics and light-curve morphology.
4. Places jet-launching in the **IMBH/dwarf regime**, complementing Swift J1644, AT 2022cmc, and AT 2018hyz.
5. Super-Eddington accretion (Ṁ/Ṁ_Edd ∼ 10³) inferred from L_g,peak ≈ 4 L_Edd, consistent with IMBH-TDE nozzle-shock predictions.

## Strengths
- Genuinely multi-wavelength: VLASS + VLA + LOFAR + RACS + ZTF + Legacy + MOSFIRE + eROSITA.
- Two well-separated radio epochs enable independent shock-parameter estimates.
- Explicit discussion of viewing-angle degeneracies (initial Γ could be ultra-relativistic; late-time data cannot constrain launch time).
- Quantitative comparison to Swift J1644, AT 2022cmc, AT 2018hyz, VT J0243, and LFBOTs.
- Balmer decrement (Hα/Hβ ≈ 9) and broad-Hα width (1900 km/s) fit the emerging radio-selected TDE emission-line pattern.

## Weaknesses
- **Single-event classification.** Jetted TDE is favoured, not uniquely determined; a BH-stellar-merger origin (Klencki & Metzger; Tsuna & Lu) is only *disfavoured*, not excluded.
- **M_BH via host scaling** carries ~2 dex uncertainty; a stellar-mass BH engine (à la Beniamini et al. for AT 2022cmc) cannot be ruled out.
- Only one X-ray epoch (upper limit at ~6 months); no ability to distinguish AT 2018hyz-type vs. VT J0243-type behavior.
- Jet launch time is degenerate over ~1000 d; energetics rely on canonical ε_e = 0.1, ε_B = 0.01.
- MOSFiT super-Eddington modification is heuristic.

## Relevance to Vojtěch
An IMBH-TDE in a dwarf galaxy directly informs the mass regime of wandering IMBHs and LISA EMRI hosts; super-Eddington accretion sustained for years is precisely the regime probed by EMRI-EM counterpart scenarios; the low z (~0.27) gives a rare bright benchmark for jet–disk connection physics in low-mass BHs; the inferred low ambient density (~0.04 cm⁻³ at R ∼ 10¹⁹ cm) is relevant for CSM/afterglow modeling around galactic nuclei.

## Scores
- **Quality:** 8/10 — solid observational case, appropriate caveats, single-event with unavoidable classification degeneracy.
- **Relevance:** 8/10 — direct hit on TDE, IMBH accretion, and EMRI EM-counterpart parameter space.
