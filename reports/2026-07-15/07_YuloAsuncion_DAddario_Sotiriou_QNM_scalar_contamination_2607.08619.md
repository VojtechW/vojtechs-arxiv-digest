# 🎵 QNM contamination by massless scalars (Horndeski EFT)

**Citation:** Miguel Yulo Asuncion, Giovanni D'Addario, Thomas P. Sotiriou, *Beyond black hole spectroscopy: Quasinormal mode contamination by massless scalars*, arXiv:[2607.08619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08619) [gr-qc, astro-ph.HE, hep-th], 11 Jul 2026.

## Executive Summary
The paper carries out a systematic perturbative expansion of shift-symmetric Horndeski gravity around a Kerr background to order O(εq²), where ε bookkeeps dynamical perturbations and q is the scalar charge per unit BH mass. It identifies which operators in the Horndeski action generate (i) the standard "frequency shift" δω of QNMs and (ii) the recently proposed "frequency contamination" (extra QNM modes from the coupled scalar field itself). Main technical result: only the linear φG Gauss-Bonnet coupling α and the leading G̃₄ term (τ₄) contribute; τ₃ and τ₅ Horndeski sectors decouple at this order. Under the natural assumption φ^(1,0)=0 (scalar amplitude q-suppressed), shift and contamination appear at the same order O(εq²) and are governed by α alone. If φ^(1,0)≠0, contamination enters at O(εq) and dominates.

## Key Contributions
- Extension of D'Addario et al. framework from O(εq) to O(εq²), reaching the same order as the theory-agnostic ansatz of Crescimbeni et al.
- Concrete match between theory-agnostic BH spectroscopy phenomenology and a well-defined EFT.
- Clean derivation that φG is the *sole* driver of the leading bGR effect; phenomenological amplitudes carry information on {α, τ₄} independently.
- Argument that q∝M⁻² makes LISA supermassive-binary ringdowns a poor probe of massless scalars, while ET/CE stellar-mass BHs will do better.

## Strengths
- Rigorous perturbative bookkeeping with explicit field equations up to O(εq²).
- Bridges "theory-agnostic + parametrized" community with EFT model builders.
- Correctly identifies structural point: contamination and shift are on equal perturbative footing.

## Weaknesses
- Stops at the *ansatz level*. No actual QNM frequencies ω̂, no numerical δω, no explicit computation of amplitudes. It is essentially a "which couplings enter where" bookkeeping exercise.
- Result that only α and τ₄ survive at O(εq²) is largely predictable from EFT dimensional scaling — incremental over prior work.
- The most interesting claim (that φ^(1,0)≠0 could make contamination dominant during nonlinear merger amplification) is qualitative and speculative.
- Restricted to shift-symmetric massless scalars — the more phenomenologically interesting scalarization cases are flagged as future work.
