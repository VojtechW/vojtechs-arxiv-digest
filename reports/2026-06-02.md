## 🧩 Thematic Trends This Week

**QPE / EMRI star-disk interaction is now an active multi-paper conversation.** Three of the survivors (Linial-Metzger-Beloborodov, Chen-You-Miniutti, Zhou-Zhang-Pan-Zhao) all sit on the same physical picture — a stellar EMRI crossing a viscously evolving accretion disk — and pull it toward different observables (delayed radio, recurrence-energy correlations via warps, O−C timing). The Chinese-language EMRI+disk school (Pan, You, Zhou) and the Princeton/Columbia school (Linial, Metzger, Beloborodov) are now both consolidating around this framework. The Arcodia+2026 vs Zhou+2025 GSN 069 / eRO-QPE2 timing dispute is open and worth tracking — this is one of those weeks where the field is converging on actual model selection rather than parameter fitting.

**Eccentric-orbit waveform modeling crosses a maturity threshold.** SEOBNRv6EHM is released along with its first LVK PE re-analysis (Gamboa et al. and Pompili et al. as companion papers). Mismatch ≲2% to NR at e ~ 0.9 plus support for dynamical capture and unbound scattering pushes EOB-family modeling into the regime that was previously the exclusive territory of NR. The first NR-injection systematics comparison across v5EHM / v6EHM / TEOB-Dalí is genuinely a first. For EMRI/IMRI tracking, the analytical-only eccentric-correction tension flagged in v6EHM (1PN truncation chosen empirically because higher-PN orders degrade waveforms while improving scattering angles) is the kind of structural issue worth watching in subsequent papers.

**Wet-EMRI population synthesis is incrementally maturing.** The Xue-Haiman-Tagawa-Bartos paper is on the suppression side (recoils kill EMRIs) while Drummond et al. is on the dynamics side (environmental forces leave a detectable signature). Both papers expose how much of LISA-rate forecasting still rests on choices in the migration/binary-formation/recoil semi-analytic machinery — a useful reminder when reading any "LISA EMRI rate is X yr⁻¹" claim.
