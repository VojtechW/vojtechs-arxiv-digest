# 🎵 Long-Lived Ringing of Near-Extremal Kerr Black Holes Resonantly Driven by EMRIs

**arXiv:** [2606.17883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17883)
**Author:** Wen-Biao Han
**Categories:** gr-qc
**Comments:** 5 pages, 3 figures

---

## Summary

Argues that bound EMRIs around near-extremal Kerr black holes can **resonantly drive** the long-lived zero-damped modes (ZDMs) of the central black hole. The author performs frequency-domain Teukolsky amplitude calculations at a = 0.9999 for eccentric-inclined geodesics on the separatrix and identifies a source-supported orbital harmonic whose real frequency falls within one pole half-width of the fundamental gravitational ZDM. A pole-fitting / "complex-response tomography" procedure recovers the independent (2,2,0) ZDM frequency to within ~3% of one pole half-width, with a coherent pole fraction P ≈ 0.6–0.9 across 12 inclination cuts.

## What's genuinely new

- **Pole tomography of EMRI-sourced Teukolsky amplitudes** isolates the ZDM pole from smooth source enhancement — a step beyond the Thornburg-Wardell-van de Meent identification of QNM "wiggles" in self-force quantities.
- **Explicit κ-scaling of the access window** in spin, mapping where in the (a, p, e, x) parameter space the orbital frequency can land within a pole half-width of a ZDM.

## Strengths

- The pole-fitting/tomography idea is methodologically clean — it disentangles the QNM contribution from the (much larger) smooth orbital amplitude.
- Working at a = 0.9999 (rather than a = 1) keeps the calculation well-defined.

## Weaknesses

- **The "resonant driving" framing overstates a sub-dominant effect.** P ≤ 1 by construction, there is no Lorentzian peak in |Z|², and the pole contribution is comparable to but not dominant over the smooth non-pole piece. This is not driving in the usual amplitude-amplification sense.
- **Single benchmark spin (a = 0.9999) and orbit on the separatrix.** The orbit-on-the-separatrix construction is geometrically the closest to where ZDMs are accessible but is also a long way from a realistic adiabatic EMRI: a true inspiral spends only a transient time near the separatrix.
- **No multi-mode Kerr Green-function calculation** — the paper explicitly defers the full Green-function construction that would be required to make the "surface gravity spectroscopy" promise quantitative.
- 5 pages with bold claims warrants extra skepticism. The headline interpretation will likely be tempered as the field reproduces and extends.
