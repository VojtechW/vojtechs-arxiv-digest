# 🕳️ Semi-Analytical Loss-Cone Theory for TDE Rates Around Kerr Black Holes

**arXiv:** [2606.18050](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18050)
**Author:** Wenkang Xin (Oxford MMathPhys dissertation, Trinity 2026; supervisor most likely Andrew Mummery)
**Categories:** astro-ph.HE, astro-ph.GA, gr-qc
**Comments:** 68 pages, 6 figures

---

## Summary

Computes inclination-dependent Kerr tidal-disruption and direct-capture boundaries **analytically**, bypassing Marck's parallel-transported tetrad via the ZAMO-frame algebraic transformation of Xin & Mummery 2025 (arXiv:2511.21499). Embeds the boundaries in a 2D Fokker-Planck equation in (L², L_z/L) at fixed energy. The headline result is a clean separation of scales: the Kerr boundary induces an **O(ε) local bias favouring retrograde disruption**, but the global TDE rate picks up only an **O(ε²) geometric correction** — so population-level TDE rates are remarkably spin-insensitive.

## What's genuinely new

- **Closed-form, inclination-dependent** capture and disruption fractions on Kerr, replacing previous numerical evaluations.
- **2D Fokker-Planck analysis** in angular-momentum direction space with nested disruption/capture boundaries; existing treatments use 1D L-only diffusion or full N-body / Monte Carlo.
- The **clean O(ε) → O(ε²) scaling argument** for the global rate is a useful structural statement.

## Strengths

- Solid analytical execution of the boundary geometry.
- The underlying methodology (Xin & Mummery 2025) is the genuinely interesting contribution — this thesis applies it to a population-level question and extracts a quantitative bottom line.
- 68 pages is thesis length but the substantive new material is well-organized; intro and review are pedagogical but not padded.

## Weaknesses

- **The "first semi-analytical framework" framing overstates novelty.** Kesden 2012 and Servin-Kesden 2017 already did spin-dependent TDE/capture boundary analyses. This work refines and analytically closes prior numerical results; the framing is slightly oversold.
- **As a dissertation, much of the structural novelty (the tidal-tensor algebraic frame transformation) lives in the companion paper Xin & Mummery 2025**, not in this document. The thesis is best read alongside that paper.
- **No comparison to observed TDE rates** (Wevers, Yao, French, Mockler) — the prediction of spin-insensitivity is a real testable claim but isn't connected to data.
- Loss-cone microphysics (resonant relaxation, secular evolution, mass segregation) is treated at the standard level; novel physics is purely in the inner boundary geometry.

## Relevance to Vojtěch

**High.** Touches all of: galactic-center dynamics, Kerr capture/disruption boundaries, the "TDE-as-EMRI-precursor" line that connects to QPE physics. The O(ε²) suppression of spin signatures in the global rate is an important null result for anyone trying to extract central-BH spin from TDE populations. The companion paper (Xin & Mummery 2025) on the ZAMO algebraic tidal tensor formalism is likely the more important reference for theoretical follow-up.
