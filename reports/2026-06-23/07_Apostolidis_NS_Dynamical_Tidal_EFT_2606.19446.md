# 〰️ Dynamical Tidal Response of Neutron Stars: from EFT to Gravitational Waveforms

**arXiv:** [2606.19446](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19446)
**Authors:** Thomas Apostolidis, Valerio De Luca, Leonardo Gualtieri, Takuya Katagiri, Paolo Pani, Luca Santoni
**Categories:** gr-qc, astro-ph.HE, hep-th
**Comments:** 30 pages, 8 figures

---

## Summary

Extends the Schwarzschild Born-series + dimensional-regularization/MS worldline-EFT framework (Combaluzier-Szteinsznaider et al.) to neutron-star interiors. The authors fix the scheme-dependent finite parts of the O(ω²) electric quadrupole coupling and add a b₀-dependent NS-specific term β₂ = −(64/45)(1 + (321/224) Λ₀) to the otherwise universal Schwarzschild RG running. They propagate the result through an 8PN tidal phase and argue ET could resolve dynamical Love numbers and that ignoring them biases static-Λ inference.

## What's genuinely new

- **First complete LO dynamical tidal corrections to both conservative dynamics and the GW signal of compact binaries** in this worldline-EFT scheme, including scheme-dependent finite parts.
- The b₀-dependent NS-specific correction β₂ adds to the otherwise universal Schwarzschild RG running — modest structural insight.
- Demonstration that "modes alone don't suffice" — the dynamical response cannot be fully captured by a sum over discrete f-mode excitations alone.

## Strengths

- Cross-checked against two independent interior formulations.
- Explicit, reproducible calculation in dim-reg/MS.
- Honest 8PN tidal-phase propagation lets the reader judge magnitude of the new piece for ET-era science.

## Weaknesses

- **The "first complete" claim is narrower than advertised.** Saketh et al. 2026 did the same calculation in parallel using off-shell methods (this paper uses on-shell), and Mandal-Steinhoff et al. 2023 already had the relevant counterterms.
- **The "modes alone don't suffice" structural point is technically right but practically a ~5% deviation** for realistic NS parameters.
- **No direct benchmark against Hinderer-Lackey TIDV or Pratten-Schmidt** — both are current state-of-the-art tidal models. The waveform-systematic claim would be much stronger with a head-to-head comparison.
- Not directly an EMRI/Kerr paper.

## Relevance to Vojtěch

**Moderate.** Useful as an example of how the EFT/RG structure on perturbed Schwarzschild generalizes when the matter sector is non-trivial. The structural lesson — that mode sums alone don't capture the full response — is conceptually relevant for any EFT-style treatment of the EMRI primary or secondary. Less directly relevant than the Solon paper.
