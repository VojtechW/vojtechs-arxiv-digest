### 📖 Should-Read (4)

#### 📜 Convergence of post-Newtonian for quasi-circular non-precessing comparable-mass-ratio BBHs
**arxiv:** [2605.20562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20562)
**Authors:** Dongze Sun, **Béatrice Bonga** (Tier 2), **Leo C. Stein** (Tier 1), Guido Da Re
**Categories:** gr-qc
**Quality 7.5/10 · Relevance 8/10**

Benchmarks PN energy flux against a 60-orbit q=3 SXS+CCE NR waveform in a BMS-fixed frame, with only (M, q) as physical fit parameters. Headline results: PN improves through 6PN for `v ≲ 0.45`; the `v^(2N+11)` error scaling holds; but improvement is **non-monotonic** with anomalous bumps at 2.5PN and 4PN — refuting the Yunes–Berti "first local minimum" optimal-truncation prescription. PN loses convergence near `v ~ 0.47` (~ICO). NR error already dominates below `v ≈ 0.32`.

**Caveats:** rests on a single q=3 simulation; the EMRI-derived 6PN flux input is incomplete; the extrapolated 10⁻¹⁵ accuracy claim is fragile. Direct comparable-mass extension of EMRI-limit PN convergence work (Sago/Fujita/Nakano, Yunes/Berti) — squarely in Vojtěch's territory.

#### ☄️ Impact of axion-like self-interactions in gravitational atoms for LISA
**arxiv:** [2605.17589](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17589)
**Authors:** Samuel Gómez Gómez, Xisco Jiménez Forteza, Carlos Palenzuela Luque
**Categories:** gr-qc, astro-ph.HE, hep-ph
**Quality 6/10 · Relevance 7/10**

Plugs Eby et al.'s self-interaction-driven (non-superradiant) ALP cloud formation into an EMRI inspiral and runs Fisher-mismatch + posterior analysis on LISA. Recovers (m_dm, f_a) at 10–40% relative precision for SNR ~ 20 in a narrow triangular window `m_dm ~ 10⁻¹⁷–10⁻¹⁵ eV`, `f_a ~ 10¹⁰–10¹² GeV`. Genuinely distinct from the superradiance + spike literature because the cloud lives in `n=1, l=0` and is grown over Hubble time from ambient ρ_dm rather than imposed. Pipeline is clean; dephasing ~9 rad with `M_cloud ~ 10⁻⁶ M_BH`; self-gravity neglect is justified.

**Caveats:** requires `t_0 ~ 0.9 t_universe` of unperturbed capture (ignores merger/AGN history); no degeneracy treatment vs gas dynamical friction, DM spikes, or eccentric inspirals (same negative-PN territory); only 2-parameter Fisher with "trust me, joint 4-param doesn't change it"; only Newtonian Chandrasekhar DF used.

**Categories:** astro-ph.HE, gr-qc
**Quality 6.5/10 · Relevance 8/10**

Three-channel dynamical BBH catalog (Galactic-field flybys + GN Kozai–Lidov + GC N-body) propagated to LISA via Peters-1964 harmonic decomposition + secular PN (a,e) evolution. Predicts `~36 / 13 / 4.7 / 2.3 / 1.0` MW BBH sources at SNR > 1 / 3 / 8 / 20 / 50 over 10 yr of LISA. Public **LEAP** code + MW catalog is a real asset.

**Caveats:** Peters + secular-PN templates are inadequate for the "highly eccentric" regime the abstract advertises; channel branching fractions are point estimates (no marginalization); LVK-band distinguishability claim lacks a Bayes-factor forecast; no explicit benchmark vs Rodriguez/Samsing/Zevin/Romero-Shaw.

#### 💫 Gravitational Wave Hyperbolic Catalog: Reanalyzing High-Mass GW Signals Using Hyperbolic Waveforms
**arxiv:** [2605.21640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21640)
**Authors:** Jacob Lange, Danilo Chiaramello, Peter Lott, Chad Henshaw, Alessandro Nagar, Richard O'Shaughnessy, Laura Cadonati
**Categories:** gr-qc
**Quality 6/10 · Relevance 8/10**

First systematic LVK-catalog hyperbolic reanalysis (38 high-mass events) using TEOBResumS-Dalí against quasi-circular precessing templates via RIFT. Only GW190521 favors hyperbolic, with `ln B = 3.71 ± 0.11` (BF ≈ 41) — **far below Gamba+2023's 4300:1**. **Caveats:** no unbound+precessing model exists (comparison is hyp+aligned vs QC+prec — apples to oranges); GW190521 favors low-`p_φ⁰` direct-plunge region where TEOB-Dalí is least reliable and uses a QC ringdown; parallel Pompili+2026 (SEOBNRv6EHM) disagrees on GW191109_010717 — exactly the waveform-systematics flag that should be more prominent. #### 🪄 Proca-type Hair of Rotating Black Holes in Higher Dimensions
**arxiv:** [2605.23077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23077)
**Authors:** David Kubizňák, Robert B. Mann, Marek Milička
**Quality 7/10 · Relevance 6/10**

Clears Vojtěch's hidden-symmetry bar — but barely. Unlike the f(R) Kerr–Newman case (2604.14066, anti-example in the preferences file), the stealth cancellation here is **not** forced by metric form: it requires the second-derivative Killing identity `∇_a∇_b ξ_c = −ξ^d R_dacb`, and the coupling β = 1 is *derived* by matching the Proca EOM to the contracted Killing integrability condition. Real structural content. But the hidden-symmetry payoff is one paper away — the current paper exploits only ordinary Killing vectors (a non-perturbative upgrade of Wald 1974 / Aliev–Frolov 2004) and a conformal-Killing extension in Appendix B. **The actual Killing–Yano version Vojtěch cares about** is announced as ref. [36] (Kubizňák–Mann–Milička, "From (hidden) symmetries to stealth solutions") — **that's the follow-up to watch**. Skeptic's caveat: "hair" is oversold — the field is invisible to thermodynamics, gauge invariance breaks the stealthiness, and the construction is fundamentally kinematic (`A_a = ξ_a`).

#### 🌌 Dynamics of Binary Systems around a Supermassive Black Hole: Binary Scattering and Eccentric vZLK Oscillations
**arxiv:** [2605.22525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22525)
**Authors:** Kei-ichi Maeda, Hirotada Okawa
**Quality 5.5/10 · Relevance 6/10**

Equatorial-Kerr COM geodesic + Newtonian binary in a Fermi frame with leading tidal `R_{0k0ℓ} r^k r^ℓ` and a 0.5PN `R_{0kjℓ}` correction. `m_{1,2} = 10 M_⊙`, `M = 10⁸ M_⊙`. Genuinely useful: a "scattering-type vZLK" picture in which eccentric outer orbits replace the secular cycle by step-like jumps at each periapsis with `Θ = √(1−e²) cos I` conserved only on average (~10% excursions). ## Thematic Trends This Week

**Ringdown is having a moment, on two fronts.** De Amicis et al. (2605.16492) and Ould El Hadj & Dolan (2605.15429) both reconfigure the QNM-sum representation of the late-time waveform — the first by carefully relaxing the high-overtone causality bound of Leaver's series so that dynamical QNMs alone reproduce the Schwarzschild plunge response, the second by exporting the optics Debye-series construction into the Fabry–Pérot cavity of horizonless echoers. Both papers are upstream of the ringdown-spectroscopy + black-hole-mimicker conversations that LVK's upcoming O5 and Einstein Telescope era will sharpen. The convergence of independent groups onto the `r* = 0` bounce-radius picture (Arnaudo–Kehagias, Bhattacharyya, Cardoso, and now De Amicis+Sberna) is the single most encouraging signal: a structural feature of plunge-driven QNM excitation that nobody saw clearly in 1990s-era plunge-QNM literature.

**Comparable-mass PN diagnostics, eccentricity, and hyperbolic encounters all push at the same edge.** Sun–Bonga–Stein–Da Re (2605.20562) document that PN convergence is genuinely worse at comparable mass than the EMRI limit, with anomalous 2.5PN/4PN bumps that prevent any simple "optimal truncation". Xuan et al. (2605.15265) catalog the eccentric BBH population that LISA will see in the milky-way + nuclear-cluster + globular-cluster channels and warn that LISA harmonics of a single source will alias as quasi-monochromatic global-fit entries. Lange et al. (2605.21640) reanalyze the LVK high-mass catalog with hyperbolic templates and find that the Gamba+2023 hyperbolic preference for GW190521 has degraded by ~100× under updated waveform systematics — a useful but not decisive update. All three of these papers are saying the same thing in different dialects: **the high-eccentricity / parabolic / unbound corner of the BBH waveform manifold is the next frontier**, and the waveform models are not yet ready.

**Environmental EMRI physics is consolidating.** Gómez–Jiménez Forteza–Palenzuela (2605.17589) is the latest variant of the "what does LISA see if there's a boson cloud around the primary" question, now with a self-interaction-driven (non-superradiant) ALP cloud grown from ambient ρ_dm. The forecast is honest enough to acknowledge `~10⁻⁶ M_BH` cloud mass and `~9 rad` dephasing in a narrow `f_a` window, but the field as a whole continues to ignore degeneracies with gas dynamical friction, eccentric inspirals, and DM-spike DF — all of which sit at the same negative-PN signature. A clean joint-degeneracy paper is overdue.
