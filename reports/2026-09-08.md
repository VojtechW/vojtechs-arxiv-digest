**🌀 Enabling gravitational-wave astronomy with spin-precessing black holes on generic orbits**
Gamboa, Pompili, Buonanno, Sebastiani, Enficiaud, Boyle, Kidder, Pfeiffer, Ramos-Buades, Scheel — [arXiv:2609.01568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01568) — gr-qc
The effective-one-body waveform family finally gets a model (SEOBNRv6EPHM) that treats orbital eccentricity and spin precession at the same time, validated against 1524 numerical-relativity simulations with median mismatches below 1%.
This is the first generic-orbit, spin-precessing SEOBNR model, closing the gap between the precessing-quasi-circular (v5PHM) and eccentric-aligned-spin (v5EHM) branches. It evolves 13 coupled ODEs, twists up six co-precessing modes, and covers bound IMR binaries, dynamical captures, and scattering. It beats the only competitor (TEOBResumS-Dalí) by a median factor ~4 in mismatch while being up to ~10× faster, so eccentric+precessing parameter estimation costs no more than current quasi-circular analyses. A proof-of-principle reanalysis of GW200129 favours eccentricity, backed by the first NR-injection recovery test for such a model.
Quality 9/10 · Relevance 5/10 · **Must-Read** (as a benchmark of state-of-the-art comparable-mass waveform modeling, not core EMRI reading)
*Caveats:* The precessing sector is validated but **not** NR-calibrated — precession enters via PN-expanded *quasi-circular* spin equations fed with eccentric dynamics, an untested extrapolation in the hardest regime; only 87 eccentric-precessing NR waveforms (q ≤ 6, e_gw ≤ 0.6), with mismatches degrading to ~10% in the high-e/high-precession/high-q corner; a known unquantified bias — the scattering test shows both EOB models overpredict polar deflection because the precession equations omit radial terms; mode asymmetries neglected, directly relevant to the GW200129 eccentricity-vs-precession call; the GW200129 evidence is soft and glitch-dependent (log₁₀ B ≈ 0.8–5.4).
**In network:** 🚩 Alessandra Buonanno, 🚩 Harald P. Pfeiffer, 🚩 Michael Boyle (Tier-3 notable authors)
*Where to start:* Fig. 2 and "Agreement with NR waveforms" (mismatch distribution, factor-4 win over Dalí), then Fig. 3 / the scattering-angle section (honest limits of the precession dynamics), then the GW200129 section + Fig. 4 and Table 1.

**🎯 Not All Resonances Are Created Equal: Prioritizing Tidal Resonances in EMRIs**
Bonga, Bourg, ten Brink, Peters — [arXiv:2609.03732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03732) — gr-qc
When a distant star or black hole tugs on a small body inspiralling into a supermassive black hole, it occasionally kicks the orbit at "resonances"; this paper maps where all such kicks occur across orbit space and ranks the few that actually matter for LISA.
Working in the weak-tide three-body hierarchy with the perturber held stationary, the authors survey tidal resonances for generic Kerr orbits, computing resonance contours in (p, e, x), durations, and jumps in L_z and Q (with ΔE = 0 fixed by time-translation symmetry). They rank the (n, k, m) modes by the phase-independent product of jump × duration: prograde is dominated by (3, −1, −1) and (3, 0, −2); retrograde shifts to (3, −1, 1) and (3, 0, 2). A Schwarzschild-tide-on-Kerr-geodesic shortcut gives a 20–50× speedup with ≲ 0.1–1% accuracy, and all data are released for FEW ingestion.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 9/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* The stationary-perturber approximation is load-bearing and the authors flag that relaxing it "introduces qualitatively new structure" that could reshuffle the ranking (deferred to a paper in preparation); no observable is actually computed — no dephasing in radians, no mismatch, no detectability threshold, only the phase-independent proxy; several acknowledged unexplained features (jumps vanishing for circular orbits, a PN5 duration spike near e ≈ 0.35, the spin/inclination jump pattern); numerical accuracy degrades exactly where jumps are largest (high e, high a); incremental in method — it operationalizes effects established by Bonga–Yang–Hughes and Gupta et al.
**In network:** 🚩 Béatrice Bonga, 🚩 Patrick Bourg (extended-network collaborators)
*Where to start:* Sec. 2.3–2.5 (resonance condition, the two key approximations), then Sec. 6 (ranking + the dynamic-perturber caveat), then skim Sec. 4–5 for duration/jump trends and the tier tables.
[Full report](2026-09-08/02_Prioritizing_tidal_resonances_in_EMRIs_2609.03732)

**💥 Unlocking the QPE Mystery: Star-Disk Collisions in Realistic AGN Disks**
Zhu, X. Huang, Jiang, S. Huang — [arXiv:2609.03011](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03011) — astro-ph.HE
2D multi-group radiation-hydro simulations of a star punching through a realistic, radiation-pressure-dominated, puffed-up accretion disk — rather than an analytic thin disk — give a geometric explanation for the ~10–20% quasi-periodic-eruption duty cycle.
Embedding the collision in the vertical/radial structure of a global radiation-MHD disk (photosphere pushed out to z/r ≈ 0.7), the supersonic star drives a bow shock whose portions break out through the scattering photosphere at different times. The flare duration is set by the shock-crossing time H_es/v_K ≈ 0.11 T_0 — reproducing the observed duty cycle geometrically and robustly across impact angle, while peak luminosity varies by >10×. The four sampled disks follow Σ ∝ r², giving a luminosity–period trend E ∝ P^(2/3) that matches the weaker half of the QPE sample.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 9/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* Cannot address the flagship near-side/far-side amplitude asymmetry — the thick disk pushes the receding photosphere off the domain, so the far-side flux is unmeasured and it is left open whether the asymmetry even survives; the disk is a 5×10⁸ M☉ AGN sim hand-rescaled by 500 (turbulence not re-run at target mass, B-fields stripped from the collision); the L–P exponent is off by ~2× from the observed 1.34–1.67; spectral temperatures miss (25–50 eV vs observed 100–200 eV) without an artificial 100×Σ disk + invoked Comptonization; single crossing, 2D, solid-sphere star, Newtonian gravity — a proof-of-concept, no fit to GSN 069 or any eRO-QPE.
*Where to start:* Sec. 3 (the duty-cycle argument and Fig. 3 light curves across incident angles), then the right panel of Fig. 4 (E–P data vs prediction) and Fig. 5 (100×Σ scaling); skim Sec. 4 for the far-side-asymmetry gap.

### 📖 Should-Read

**🎢 Tidally-enhanced resonances in extreme-mass-ratio inspirals: A tertiary path to chaos**
Destounis, Katagiri, Mukherjee, Kokkotas — [arXiv:2609.03007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03007) — gr-qc, astro-ph.HE, nlin.CD
Geodesics around a Kerr black hole deformed by a frozen external quadrupolar tidal field lose integrability, and the Poincaré-map islands / rotation-curve plateaus are shown to be exactly the Gupta–Bonga tidal resonances, via an action-angle phase-locking analysis.
The paper's real contribution is the conceptual bridge: it demonstrates that the Destounis-style chaos diagnostics and the action-angle tidal-resonance formalism describe the same physics — the finite-width plateaus *are* the phase-space footprint of tidal resonances. It uses a properly constructed relativistic tidally-deformed Kerr metric, scans tidal orientation, and identifies which resonant angles phase-lock inside the plateaus (including a "pseudo-resonance" 5/7 mode that circulates without secular effect). Reads naturally alongside the Bonga–Bourg paper above.
Quality 6/10 · Relevance 9/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* Strongly incremental over the group's own prior Katagiri et al. setup (Poincaré maps "also shown in" that reference); chaos is asserted structurally, not quantified (no Lyapunov exponents — the chaotic orbits are "not visible" in the figures); purely conservative frozen-field geodesics with no radiation reaction, so the GW-glitch/dephasing claims are extrapolation; the action-angle diagnostic uses *unperturbed* Kerr variables with validity only asserted; some overselling (ε^{1/2} scaling called "logarithmic"; florid intro, ~200-ref citation dump).
*Where to start:* Figs. 2/7 (rotation-curve plateaus) with Fig. 10 and Sec. IV.1 (action-angle libration vs circulation) — this pairing is the novelty; then Eq. 20 (orbit–tide coupling) and the validity hierarchy Eqs. 17–19.

**🧭 A Unified Treatment of the Self-Force Problem**
Modrekiladze, Rothstein, Wilson-Gerow — [arXiv:2609.01644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01644) — gr-qc, astro-ph.HE, hep-th
A worldline-EFT + closed-time-path master formula for the leading environmental self-force (dynamical friction), with the striking structural result that Chandrasekhar's Coulomb logarithm is really a renormalization-group log whose coefficient is universal.
Despite the sweeping title, this does **not** unify the vacuum GSF formalisms — "self-force" is broadened to dynamical friction in a matter environment (dust, fluid, scalar dark matter), all handled by one covariant one-loop CTP computation via the medium's retarded stress-energy correlator. The genuine payoffs are structural: the Coulomb log is a bona-fide UV divergence with a proven universal coefficient, and a conservative coupling sources a dissipative counterterm with β_K ∝ M². Relevant to EMRI environmental-dephasing systematics for LISA/ET.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 6/10 · **Should-Read** (for the EFT/structural axis)
*Caveats:* The title is oversold — no vacuum GSF, no second order, no singular-regular split; framework-plus-validation only, every example *reproduces* known results and the exciting deliverables (finite-size, arbitrary trajectories, the RG flow, accretion) are deferred; the "universal log" partly restates a known prefactor universality — the new content is the RG *interpretation*; the RG flow is written down, not demonstrated to have any observable consequence; leading order in G only; the EFT/response-function framing was independently in the air at submission.
*Where to start:* §1 + §1.1 (scope, why vacuum has no log), then §5 (the f-sum-rule proof of log universality — the core payload) and the Conclusions; §3.2 for the key CTP move.

### 🔍 Worth-Skimming

**🔮 Zero-damped modes of near-extremal Reissner–Nordström black holes from exact WKB**
Lo Chiatto, Schenk, Wagner, Yu — [arXiv:2609.02816](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02816) — hep-th, gr-qc, math-ph
A clean proof-of-concept that pushes the near-extremal RN zero-damped-mode spectrum two orders beyond the known leading term using an all-orders-in-η exact-WKB (Voros-symbol) treatment, validated against Leaver's method.
Its value is the *mechanism*: it makes explicit why naive photon-sphere WKB fails for zero-damped modes (the near-horizon confluence turns the horizon into a double pole with an infinitely-winding Stokes spiral) and how the near-horizon throat controls the spectrum. Notably **not** a routine "QNM of exotic metric X" paper — the metric is standard and the point is the method and the analytic structure.
Quality 6/10 · Relevance 7/10 · **Worth-Skimming**
*Caveats:* The concrete new physics is thin — two higher-order corrections (ε², ε⁴) for the simplest scalar on the simplest two-horizon metric, self-described as a proof of concept; the deepest promised connections (Seiberg–Witten periods, resurgence) are flagged and then not made; not fully rigorous (one Voros symbol is evaluated only at logarithmic accuracy with an undetermined O(1) prefactor); a crowded niche — Hatsuda–Shiga and Miyachi et al. did closely related exact-WKB-QNM work months earlier; RN is a mathematically convenient toy with negligible astrophysical charge.
*Where to start:* Sec. 5.1–5.3 (Voros-symbol computation, the enhancement collapsing the quantization condition to V₁₂ = −1, the central spectrum Eq. 5.23), then Conclusions for the honest scope; skim Sec. 2.2 for the double-pole Stokes-spiral picture.

## 🗑️ Papers That Failed Critical Review

1. **Charged black-hole binary radiation at second post-Newtonian order** ([2609.02825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02825), Placidi, Grilli, Pegorin, Orselli). CUT — scored below the shortlist threshold. A methodologically solid computation: the first 2PN radiative sector (flux + waveform modes ℓ=2–6 + new EM-sourced memory) for non-spinning charged (Einstein–Maxwell) binaries, cross-validated by two independent formalisms (MPM-PN and EFT/NRGR agree exactly, with Ward-identity checks) and reducing correctly to the neutral 2PN and charged 1PN limits. What holds it back: the astrophysical motivation is boilerplate (BHs discharge; the "proxy for a dark U(1)" argument is never mapped onto a concrete model or a constraint forecast); pure Einstein–Maxwell (no dilaton) is in some respects a restriction of the EMD framework already treated at 1PN; the 2PN validation is entirely in-house; and the scope is narrow (non-spinning, quasi-circular). Solid but incremental and niche. (Quality 7/10, relevance 5/10.)

2. **Frequency-domain extended-effective-source gravitational self-force for eccentric Schwarzschild orbits** ([2609.02635](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02635), Lu, Gong, Zhang, Zhang, Zhou). CUT — did not clear the quality bar. A competent first gravitational frequency-domain extended-effective-source pipeline for eccentric Schwarzschild orbits, correctly targeting the real branch-switching/Gibbs obstruction and demonstrably curing it (Chebyshev coefficients to roundoff by j ≈ 20). But the method is a combination of three pre-existing ingredients (Leather–Warburton scalar EES + the group's own gravitational puncture + Wardell–Warburton circular frequency-domain source); it reproduces already-known first-order self-force values to only ~0.1–1%, with a single validation orbit (p, e) = (10, 0.3), no error budget, and no eccentricity survey. The payoff (enabling second-order eccentric frequency-domain GSF) is entirely a future promise. High core-topic relevance kept it in contention, but on quality it is an incremental proof-of-method. (Quality 5/10, relevance 8/10.)

3. **Introducing SINFONIA: Symplectic, slimplectic and Magnusian (Neural) Flows for Orbital Numerical Integration** ([2609.03329](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03329), Gomes Da Silva). CUT — proof-of-concept on a toy system; flagged for the network. The honest and genuinely interesting finding is analytic, not neural: on Tsang et al.'s 2.5PN two-body toy, an exponentiated first-order Magnusian map plus a force-law "secular-channel" shell projection beats the slimplectic baseline by ~4–5 orders of magnitude with *nothing learned*, and the paper candidly shows the neural component adds little. But the only testbed is a Newtonian + 2.5PN, planar, quasi-circular, symmetric-mass-ratio toy — no Kerr, no geodesics, no self-force, exactly the regimes where the single-secular-channel reduction is admitted not to hold; the "acceleration" claim has no end-to-end cost accounting; and several results lean on unpublished companions. The transferable secular-channel insight is worth knowing. **In network:** 🚩 Lidia J. Gomes Da Silva (extended-network collaborator). (Quality 6/10, relevance 6/10.)

4. **Electromagnetic alignment and jet precession around supermassive black holes: QPOs in tidal disruption events** ([2609.05350](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05350), Amaro-Seoane, Lui, Torres-Orjuela, Chen). FAIL — the central calculation is never shown. A 7-page proposal that an electromagnetic back-reaction torque from a Blandford–Znajek jet, added to Lense–Thirring precession of a torn mini-disk, produces a distinctive non-monotonic QPO period drift and amplitude freeze-out. The observational signature is a nice idea, but the one original computation that would justify the whole mechanism — the O(a²) split-monopole horizon torque yielding the coefficients C_align, C_prec — is asserted with no derivation, no numbers, and no appendix; there is an internal tension (BZ power ∝ a² requires high spin, yet the torque uses a small-spin expansion); no real TDE is fit; and the closely related *observed* disk–jet co-precession result (Wang et al. 2025) is not engaged. A conceptual sketch dressed in equations. (Quality 3/10, relevance 5/10.)

## 🌌 Thematic Trends This Week

**Tidal resonances in EMRIs converged from two directions.** The two strongest core-topic papers this week — Bonga, Bourg et al. ([2609.03732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03732)) and Destounis et al. ([2609.03007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03007)) — are complementary halves of the same subject, posted a day apart. The former is a practical, data-releasing survey that ranks which tidal resonances matter for LISA; the latter is a phase-space/dynamical-systems study that identifies the resonances with Poincaré-map plateaus and shows, via action-angle phase-locking, which commensurabilities drive the secular jumps. Read together they connect the "engineering" and "integrability" views of the same effect, and both lean on the Gupta–Bonga–Chua–Tanaka action-angle formalism. Both also share the same present limitation: neither yet propagates the resonant kicks into an actual observable (dephasing, mismatch), and both hold the perturber or tidal field frozen. This is a subfield maturing from existence-proofs toward waveform-pipeline readiness, but not there yet.

**Waveform modeling for "everything at once" is arriving.** SEOBNRv6EPHM ([2609.01568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01568)) is the comparable-mass counterpart of the same trend: the field is closing the last empty cell of the eccentricity × precession grid, with the eccentric-precessing corner now validated (if not calibrated) against NR. The recurring caveat across all of this generic-orbit work — EMRI and comparable-mass alike — is that the eccentric+precessing regime is where models are simultaneously most needed and least anchored to first-principles or NR input.

**Structural/analytic methods keep migrating from hep-th into gravitational dynamics.** Three of this week's survivors and near-misses are exact/EFT-structural: the worldline-CTP recasting of dynamical friction's Coulomb log as an RG flow ([2609.01644](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01644)), the exact-WKB/Voros-symbol treatment of near-extremal QNMs ([2609.02816](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02816)), and the 2PN charged-binary radiation computed twice (MPM-PN and NRGR) as a mutual check ([2609.02825](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02825)). The healthy pattern is dual-formalism validation and genuine structural reinterpretation; the recurring weakness is that the most exciting promised payoffs (RG consequences, Seiberg–Witten/resurgence links, dark-sector constraints) are consistently deferred to future work.
