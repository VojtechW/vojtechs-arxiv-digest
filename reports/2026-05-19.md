### 🥈 Should-Read

#### 📐 Analytical Fluxes from Generic Schwarzschild Geodesics
**[arXiv:2605.13847](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13847)** — Khalaf, Kavanagh, Telem
*One-line:* Closed-form Teukolsky $Z^+_{nlm}$ amplitudes for eccentric Schwarzschild orbits via a Schwarzschild→Kepler kernel + Chebyshev/Gegenbauer expansion — exact in $e$ (PN-truncated in the homogeneous Teukolsky solution).
- **Quality 7.5/10 · Relevance 8/10**
- Genuinely solves the eccentricity-expansion bottleneck that has constrained analytic EMRI flux work (Munna, Sago-Fujita). Method is exact in $e$ for the orbit-dependent kernel; PN truncation enters only through the Teukolsky homogeneous solution.
- *Caveats:* horizon fluxes deferred; Kerr extension advertised but not done; "$O(1)$ faster than BHPT" performance claim is vague.
- *Collaborator flag:* Chris Kavanagh (T2); Adam Pound and Leor Barack acknowledged.
- [Full report](2026-05-19/02_Analytical_Fluxes_Schwarzschild_2605.13847)

#### 🔧 Efficient and Stable Computation of GW Fluxes from Generic Kerr Orbits via a Unified HeunC Framework
**[arXiv:2605.09250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09250)** — Chen, Cao, Jing
*One-line:* Unified HeunC representation of both angular and radial Teukolsky equations in Kerr; Motygin's connection-coefficient algorithm eliminates the $\nu$-search that bottlenecks MST/Nekrasov-Shatashvili.
- **Quality 7/10 · Relevance 8/10**
- $\nu$-elimination is a real engineering win. Benchmarked against GSN.jl, pybhpt, BHPToolkit-MST, Nekrasov-Shatashvili. Code released (GWFluxHeunC on GitHub).
- *Caveats:* "double precision only" benchmark stacks the deck vs. arbitrary-precision MST; no comparison vs. Fujita-Hikida-Tagoshi production MST; 168-mode benchmark is restricted to $l\leq5$.
- *Collaborator flag:* Z. Nasipak (pybhpt) and R. K. L. Lo (GSN.jl) are direct benchmark targets.
- [Full report](2026-05-19/03_HeunC_Kerr_Fluxes_2605.09250)

#### ⚖️ Conservative and Dissipative Sectors in a Nonlinear Scalar Model for the Gravitational Self-Force Problem
**[arXiv:2605.14958](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14958)** — Blanco, Flanagan, Harte
*One-line:* First explicit Hamiltonian formulation of three candidate second-order self-force conservative/dissipative splits (using nonlinear scalar toy model), with IR-divergence demonstration for the iterated-time-even option.
- **Quality 7/10 · Relevance 7/10**
- Clean technical execution combining Harte's nonlocal-Hamiltonian construction with the field-mapping dressing of self-force. Cites Vojtěch's spinning-particle Hamiltonian paper (Witzany-Steinhoff-Lukes-Gerakopoulos) as motivation.
- *Caveats:* Bound orbits — the EMRI-relevant case — excluded due to IR divergences. Scalar toy model only. Existence of the required fully-symmetric 3-point function is *assumed*, never constructed.
- *Collaborator flags:* Éanna Flanagan (T3), Francisco Blanco (T2), Abraham Harte (T2); Pound and Barack acknowledged.
- [Full report](2026-05-19/01_Self_Force_Conservative_Dissipative_2605.14958)

#### 🔮 Pole Skipping, Avoided Crossing, and Resonant Excitation in Kerr QNMs near Algebraically Special Frequencies
**[arXiv:2605.17840](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17840)** — Kubota, Motohashi
*One-line:* Resolves the ~20-year-old anomalous-behavior puzzle of Kerr QNMs near algebraically special frequencies as avoided crossing (bifurcation) + Matsubara-mode pole-skipping (disappearance).
- **Quality 7.5/10 · Relevance 7/10**
- Structural / analytic-structure work, *not* an exotic-background QNM exercise. Imports pole-skipping language from holography to Kerr ringdown — genuine cross-pollination between hep-th and gr-qc. Resolves a long-standing Maassen van den Brink puzzle.
- *Caveats:* PRL-style 4-page format; verification requires recomputing Green-function building blocks. Whether the resonant-excitation enhancement is observationally large is not quantified.
- *Companion:* Motohashi has a closely-related replacement 2512.02110 with Panosso Macedo on Exceptional Points (see Notable Updates).
- [Full report](2026-05-19/09_Pole_Skipping_Kerr_QNM_2605.17840)

#### 🎵 Dynamical Quasinormal Mode Excitation II: Propagation and Convergence in Schwarzschild
**[arXiv:2605.16492](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16492)** — De Amicis, Cannizzaro, Carullo, Kuntz, Sberna
*One-line:* Bounce-radius $r_*=0$ characterization for QNM excitation during Schwarzschild plunge, with a quasi-resonant → free-oscillator regime transition that explains why QNM sums converge slowly near the peak and rapidly later.
- **Quality 7/10 · Relevance 7/10**
- Genuine sequel that adds new structural content over Paper I. Quasi-resonant → free-oscillator transition is a real explanation, not just a description.
- *Caveats:* Schwarzschild only; particle plunge with no self-force; post-peak only.
- *Collaborator flag:* Laura Sberna (T2).
- [Full report](2026-05-19/10_Dynamical_QNM_Excitation_II_2605.16492)

- *Collaborator flag:* Michal Zajaček (T2).

#### 🧩 Metric Reconstruction for Generic Black-Hole Perturbations
**[arXiv:2605.11080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11080)** — Li, Yunes
*One-line:* Extends Loutrel-Ripley NP hierarchical metric reconstruction to non-vacuum sources by adding a trace transport equation ($h_{m\bar m}$ from $\Phi_{22}$), lifting the IRG/ORG tracefree restriction.
- **Quality 6/10 · Relevance 7/10**
- Clean conceptual move: trace recovered locally from one extra transport equation, simpler than corrector-tensor approaches.
- *Caveats:* Validation example is trivial (spherical static thin shell, monopole-only). No Kerr point-particle source. Genuine novelty is the synthesis of two existing ideas (Loutrel-Ripley + Suvorov), not a breakthrough.
- *Collaborator flag:* Nicolás Yunes (T3).
- [Full report](2026-05-19/04_Metric_Reconstruction_Generic_2605.11080)

## 🌐 Thematic Trends This Week

**Eccentric-orbit Teukolsky-flux machinery is heating up.** Two papers (Khalaf-Kavanagh-Telem, 2605.13847; Chen-Cao-Jing, 2605.09250) attack the eccentric-orbit Teukolsky flux problem from different angles — analytic (Schwarzschild→Kepler kernel + Chebyshev expansion, exact in $e$) and numerical (unified HeunC + Motygin connection-coefficient algorithm eliminating the $\nu$-search). Both are tool papers; both have associated public code or roadmaps. The community appears to be converging on the realization that the small-$e$ expansion has been over-relied on, and that both analytical exactness in $e$ and numerical efficiency at moderate-to-high $e$ are achievable with the right special-function technology. This is a healthy trend for EMRI waveform modeling at the precision LISA will demand.

**Structural / analytic insight into the Kerr QNM spectrum.** The Kubota-Motohashi paper (2605.17840) on pole skipping and avoided crossings near algebraically special frequencies, together with the De Amicis-Cannizzaro-Carullo-Kuntz-Sberna sequel on dynamical QNM excitation (2605.16492), and the closely-related Panosso-Macedo et al. replacement on exceptional points in ringdown (2512.02110 — see Notable Updates), suggest a coherent community effort to put QNM excitation, propagation, and convergence on rigorous analytic-structure foundations rather than treating QNMs as a black-box numerical exercise. This is exactly the kind of structural work Vojtěch's preferences flag as qualifying for the broader-net axis.

**Galactic Center remains a battleground for observational and theoretical work on compact-object populations and SMBH environments.** Three astro-ph papers this week touched the GC dark cluster (2605.14017), Sgr A* mm variability (2605.11698), and stellar transits of nearby AGN (2605.15145). None of them deliver a single decisive new measurement or theoretical prediction, but together they outline the next decade's observational landscape for EMRI-counterpart science: ELT/MICADO for the stellar/compact-object population, ALMA/EHT for hotspot variability, and mm/VLBI for stellar eclipses of nearby radio AGN. Vojtěch's QPE-as-EMRI-counterpart program sits at the centre of this landscape and benefits from each strand.
