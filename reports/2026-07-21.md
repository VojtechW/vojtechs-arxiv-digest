**⭐ Discovery of a star sensitive to the spin of Sgr A***
GRAVITY Collaboration — [arXiv:2607.12664](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12664) — astro-ph.GA
New S-star S301 identified with the GRAVITY-RESOLVE imaging pipeline: P = 8.68 ± 0.11 yr (shortest known), e ≈ 0.983, peak orbital velocity ≈ 0.085 c, perihelion ≈ 140 R_S. Schwarzschild precession fit yields f_SP = 0.94 ± 0.88 (β²-order); the paper's own forecast is that combined GRAVITY+/ELT monitoring 2026–2035 will separate χ = 1 from χ = 0 at >5σ via Lense-Thirring drift.
Quality 9/10 · Relevance 10/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* two orientation solutions remain degenerate absent radial velocity (ERIS attempt failed); Newtonian confusion from stellar-mass perturbers up to ×10 the spin signal.
[Full report](2026-07-21/04_S301_Sgr_A_Spin_Star_2607.12664)

**🎯 High-order gravitational late-time tails in Kerr spacetime**
Casals, Kavanagh, Neef, Ottewill — [arXiv:2607.12151](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12151) — gr-qc
Closes a well-defined gap: prior Kerr-tail literature delivered only leading-order tails, and only for spherical modes with r, r′ ≫ M. Here the MST small-frequency / branch-cut-on-NIA machinery is pushed to gravitational perturbations (s = −2), at generic ℓ, m and generic r ∈ (r₊, ∞), across three asymptotic regions, with log and ln² corrections resolved. Two ancillary BHPT-integrated Mathematica notebooks are shipped.
Quality 8.5/10 · Relevance 9/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* still restricted to test-field perturbations of vacuum Kerr; second-order/self-consistent extension deferred.
**Collaborator:** 🚩 Chris Kavanagh (Tier 2).
[Full report](2026-07-21/02_Kerr_Late_Time_Tails_2607.12151)

**💥 Star-Disk Collisions II: Debris Stream Dynamics and Implications for QPEs and Other Transients Near SMBHs**
Yao, Quataert, Jiang, Linial — [arXiv:2607.08823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08823) — astro-ph.HE
First 3D Athena++ simulations of a QPE star-disk collision with the SMBH tidal + Coriolis + centrifugal potential, tracking debris out of the Hill sphere into a triaxial stream. Delivers exactly the numerics that Paper I promised and that Linial–Metzger–Quataert 2025 parametrised analytically. Reproduces the ~10–20% duty cycle observed across a decade in QPE period; unbound fraction of the shocked debris is a few percent.
Quality 8.5/10 · Relevance 9/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* no radiation transport (efficiency is a plugged-in 0.1); circular orbits only, so the eccentric long/short pattern is deferred to Paper III.
[Full report](2026-07-21/03_Star_Disk_Collisions_II_QPE_2607.08823)

**🎯 The significance of first post-adiabatic contributions for scalar charge measurements with IMRIs and EMRIs**
Barsanti, Burke, Maselli, Sotiriou, Spiers, Warburton — [arXiv:2607.09310](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09310) — gr-qc
Hybrid template combining 1PA gravitational sector (Wardell–Pound–Warburton–Miller–Durkan–Le Tiec type post-adiabatic scheme, quasi-circular Schwarzschild) with leading-order scalar flux and dipolar scalar radiation carried by a secondary of scalar charge d. Bayesian injection–recovery walks back earlier "secondary-spin-detectable" claims, showing that missing 1PA effects bias scalar-charge inference and that scalar charge is correlated with secondary spin.
Quality 8/10 · Relevance 10/10 · **Must-Read**
*Caveats:* still Schwarzschild primary, quasi-circular; second-order scalar sector not included.
**Collaborators:** 🚩🚩🚩🚩 Barsanti, Burke, Spiers, Warburton — four Tier 2 authors on one paper.
[Full report](2026-07-21/01_Post_Adiabatic_Scalar_Charge_IMRI_EMRI_2607.09310)

### 🌠 Should-Read

**🌊 Comparing Twist-Free Axisymmetric Gravitational Waves Near the Black Hole Threshold**
Adhikari, Ledvinka, Cors, Baumgarte, Khirnov, Brügmann, Hilditch — [arXiv:2607.10843](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10843) — gr-qc
Three independent NR codes (bamps, prague, sphGR) on four families of axisymmetric-wave collapse. First time-asymmetric bamps runs show *no* origin-accumulation of curvature peaks — undermining the DSS-at-origin picture in that family. Family-dependent Kretschmann scaling exponents (γ_TA ≈ 0.51, γ_B ≈ 0.23). Honest self-critique of bamps gauge failures.
Quality 7.5/10 · Relevance 6/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* fine-tuning limited to ~7 decimals (spherical work reaches 12+); local Prague-group interest is the main draw for Witzany.
**Collaborators:** 🚩 Tomáš Ledvinka (Tier 2); Anton Khirnov (Prague, adjacent).
[Full report](2026-07-21/05_Axisymmetric_GW_BH_Threshold_2607.10843)

**🌌 Dynamics in Nuclear Stellar Clusters: The Impact of Collisions and Disrupted Binaries**
Rom & Sari — [arXiv:2607.13152](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13152) — astro-ph.GA
Timescale-balance ledger: Hills injection ↔ collisions produces a universal n(r) ∝ r^{−5/4} inner density profile, insensitive to whether GW or two-body scattering dominates the inspiral. Sharpest new claim — stellar EMRIs collide before circularizing, challenging stellar-QPE models. Companion paper to the S301 discovery (Sari is co-author on both); provides the interpretive frame for S301's Hills-orbit origin.
Quality 7.5/10 · Relevance 8/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* pure analytic scaling — no Fokker–Planck, no N-body; equal solar-mass binaries only; resonant relaxation hand-waved; purely Newtonian.
[Full report](2026-07-21/08_Nuclear_Stellar_Cluster_Dynamics_2607.13152)

**⏱ Observations of X-ray Quasi-Periodic Eruptions**
Wevers, Chakraborty, Quintin, Zajaček, Giustini — [arXiv:2607.15359](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15359) — astro-ph.HE
Handbook chapter reviewing the 13-source QPE population, timing/spectral properties, host demographics (post-starburst overrepresentation, extended emission-line regions), and observational tests of both major model families (EMRI star-disk collision vs radiation-pressure disk instability). Contains one genuinely new observational result — EELR mapping of eRASSt J234403 (f_EELR = 0.69) — and a quantitative LISA-band "sweet spot" derivation (P = 0.41–1.16 hr) for future QPE–GW multimessenger candidates.
Quality 7/10 · Relevance 8/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* derivative review by design; theory side deferred to sister chapters. Useful desk reference rather than headline reading.
**Collaborator:** 🚩 Michal Zajaček (Tier 2, Prague/Brno proximity).
[Full report](2026-07-21/06_X_ray_QPE_Observations_Review_2607.15359)

**🪄 Separating the linearized Einstein equations in Kerr**
Jianwei Mei (single author) — [arXiv:2607.16581](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16581) — gr-qc
Uses a Killing–Yano–derived commuting operator plus trace/parity/de Donder conditions to produce an explicit *de Donder gauge* closed-form metric perturbation in terms of two Teukolsky-type master functions. Genuine hidden-symmetry use in a case where the separation is not a priori obvious; sidesteps the radiation-gauge singularities that plague Chrzanowski/Kegeles–Cohen reconstruction.
Quality 6.5/10 · Relevance 7/10 · **Should-Read**
*Caveats:* vacuum only; relies on an ansatz whose full generality is not proven; no numerical benchmark against Berens–Gravely–Lupsasca 2024 or Hollands–Toomani 2024/2026. Structural insight is real but the paper needs a second round of vetting from the reconstruction community.
[Full report](2026-07-21/07_Separating_Linearized_Einstein_Kerr_2607.16581)

## Thematic Trends This Week

**Galactic Center as a laboratory.** The week's marquee result is the GRAVITY collaboration's S301 discovery: a new S-star whose 8.7-yr orbit and near-radial eccentricity finally push into the parameter box needed to bound the Sgr A* spin. That paper does not itself detect the spin — Kerr β³ effects remain in a forecast — but it hands the community a decadal target that requires exactly the ingredients Witzany works on (osculating-element expansions, Newtonian-perturber confusion mitigation, and, at higher order, a self-consistent Kerr geodesic + resonant-relaxation treatment). Rom & Sari's companion NSC paper, arriving the same week, hands the interpretive frame: S301 sits close to the dominant Hills orbit predicted by their r^{−5/4} density profile. Together the two papers make a testable statement about the sEMRI-vs-collision competition inside a parsec.

**Post-adiabatic self-force is now a template-level tool.** The Barsanti–Burke–Maselli–Sotiriou–Spiers–Warburton paper is the first study to combine 1PA gravitational self-force with a scalar sector inside a full Bayesian LISA parameter-estimation pipeline. That the earlier "secondary spin is measurable" claims must be walked back once 1PA systematics are included is a significant recalibration — it says the field has moved past the era where adiabatic templates can be treated as sufficient for scalar-charge / secondary-spin science with LISA. Combined with Casals–Kavanagh–Neef–Ottewill's high-order Kerr-tail computation (which explicitly targets the Kerr Love-number and self-force communities as consumers), the tone of this week's self-force output is one of consolidation and precision, not new formalism.

**QPE physics is filling in details.** Yao–Quataert–Jiang–Linial deliver the 3D hydrodynamic completion of the Linial–Metzger–Quataert star-disk collision model, and Wevers et al.'s handbook chapter provides the observational scoreboard for both model families. The two together give a clean snapshot of a maturing subfield: the analytic model is being validated in 3D, the host-demographic and timing statistics are being assembled, and the LISA-band cross-check is now sharply forecast. What is *not* here yet is a demonstration of eccentric-orbit QPE modelling — Yao et al. flag it explicitly as Paper III — which is exactly where Witzany's spinning-particle-in-Kerr machinery would enter.
